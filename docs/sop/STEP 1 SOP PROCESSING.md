##  Activity 1.2: Approval for Outright purchase of tenancy and Debottar land (land recorded in favour of deity)

Purchase of land is to be carried out by registration of sale deed. Quantum of land is to be restricted within the limit provided in approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report with respect to the concerned Mine/ Project. Procedure of obtaining approval for purchase of land for any Mine/ Project is mostly guided by 'Uniform Guideline' circulated through office order (Ref.No. ECL/CMD/LRE/ANG/Dir. Purch. /245 dt. 22.3.2005 and subsequent amendment of the said guideline approved by FDs' vide Ref. No. ECL/CS/27 (O) /7598 dated 20.11.2009 and ECL/CS/27 (S) /8th of 2012/30 Date 10.11.2012). Some minor modifications have been incorporated in this SOP in view of change in the laws and land acquisition scenario.

As per the said Guideline a proposal for the purpose of approval for purchase of land is to be initiated by the concerned Mine/ Project. Activities at various levels are as under:

##  Colliery/ Project Level:

a) Purchase proposal shall be initiated at colliery or project level with recommendation & justification of surveyor/ survey officer, Manager, Agent or Project officer. This proposal shall be accompanied with filled up Check-list and associated documents which has been circulated through Office Order (Ref. No. ECL/CMD/LRE/ANG/Check-list/1071 Date 25thNov 2005) signed by above official.

(b) This proposal shall clearly indicate that whether employment will be provided package deal system or tagging and nos. of employment to be provided for the same.

(c) A'FIVE POINT CERTIFICATE' in Form-XVI is to be furnished with signature of Survey officer, Manager, Agent, Estate Officer or Area Survey Officer.

d) One copy of the Mouza plan showing the outline of the panel to be depillared or the area of land required for other purposes with plots clearly marked. Status of adjacent plots if purchased then when purchased etc. in separate colour wash. The plan should be signed by surveyor, Manager & Agent with noting that the outline of the panel as shown in the plan is truly superimposed with the u/g position of the panel and the plan shall indicate the following:

i. Name of the Area & project/ Mine Establishment.

ii. Mining Lease Boundary limit of land boundary / acquired up to the final stage of operation or execution of the project.

iii. Boundary of the acquired land or the land under acquisition.

iv. Mining workings indicating the boundary of workings at 5 years interval and limit of mine working at the final stage of operation.

v. Limit of subsidence and other disturbance at the surface.

vi. Types of land in different colours, Red, Blue, and Green for Forest land, Govt. land and Tenancy land respectively.

vii. G.M.K. (Gair Majurwa Khaas) land settled in the name of tenants after acquisition of land or during the process of acquisition.

viii. Infrastructure such as village, road, overhead line, colony etc.

ix. Land earmarked for forest growth and compensatory afforestation.

x. Any other detail which is relevant on the subject.

e) Two copies of schedule of land in tabular form with details like State, District, Police Station, Block, Mouza, Plot no., land type, Total area of the plot, Area acquired earlier by ECL, proposed area for purchase, remarks, if any etc along with land type wise and mouza wise abstract.

f) A Techno-economic Report regarding purchase of the land along with the following documents:

i) Calculation Sheet showing cost of land acquisition including cost of registration and mutation, Transfer value of Government land, if involved etc;

(ii) Documents to substantiate the rate of land value per acre considered in Techno Economic Report/ Calculation Sheet

iii) Documents to substantiate the rate of transfer value per acre for the Government Land, and/ or Forest Land, if the proposal involves acquisition of such Land; (If not, then clearly mention in the proposal that acquisition of no such land is involved)

(iv) Minutes of Meeting with villagers or some other documents to show that the rate of negotiated land value is acceptable to the landowners so that no complicacy arises after approval of the proposal.

(g) Copies of statutory clearances like approval obtained from DGMS, concerned Environment or Forest Department etc.

h) If the plot wise details of the land to be acquired has not been reconciled with adjacent mines/ Areas earlier then, the related areas of plots are to be reconciled with adjacent mines/ Areas which share common boundary with the mine acquiring the land and reconciliation certificates are to be prepared and signed in 'FORM-VII'.

(i) Copy of permission obtained from concerned State Government Authority/ Judicial Authority for purchase of tribal land or debottar land.

j) Duly filled in Checklist CL-1.2 and any additional document as per the said checklist is to be attached.

##  Activity 1.3: Approval for acquisition of land and Payment of Compensation as per provisions under RFCTLARR Act, 2013. (Follow Process Flow: PF-5 for guidance)

Acquisition of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 will be done by Gazette Notifications according to the statutory provisions and procedures as laid down in the Act. Quantum of land to be acquired should be limited to the provision of the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report of the Mine/ Project. If particulars of the land to be acquired under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 is not clearly mentioned in the said Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report then, a specific approval of CMD ECL is to be obtained by initiating a proposal for obtaining approval with respect to acquisition of specified quantum of land under the said Act. The proposal is to be initiated by the concerned Mine/ Project Authority mentioning purpose and justification of proposed acquisition of land and attaching documents as per CHECK LIST NO. 1.3 like copies of relevant pages of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report; copies of statutory clearances like approval obtained from DGMS, concerned Environment or Forest Department etc, a revenue plan showing boundary of the approved/ authorized project/ working area, important surface features, land owned by ECL, Government land, Patta Land, and area of land to be acquired for the workings as well as other ancillary activities approved for the project/ mine washed in different colours; Two copies of Schedule of land in tabular form with land type wise and mouza wise abstract; a 'FIVE POINT CERTIFICATE in Form- XVI, A standard 'Check List' as circulated through Office Order (Ref. No. ECL/CMD/LRE/ANG/Check-list/1071 Date 25th Nov 2005) and related documents as mentioned in the said check-list, involvement of employments and a 'Techno-Economic Report' showing estimated financial involvement along with calculation and documents to substantiate the considered cost parameters. If the plot wise details of the land to be acquired has not been reconciled with adjacent mines/ Areas earlier then, the related areas of plots are to be reconciled with adjacent mines/ Areas which share common boundary with the mine acquiring the land and reconciliation certificates are to be prepared and signed in 'FORM-VII'. These reconciliation certificates are also to be attached to the proposal. If rehabilitation of Project Affected Families is proposed then all the information and documents as mentioned in CHECK LIST NO. 3 as mentioned are to be furnished and requisite documents to substantiate the information are to be attached.

The land acquisition proposal along with the rehabilitation programme is to be examined and recommended by 'Area Land Cell' (in FORM- XXII) and by the Area General Manager. Then, the proposal is to be forwarded to General Manager (LRE), Head Quarter. He will forward the proposal to General Manager (Planning), General Manager (Safety) and General Manager (Finance) for examination and recommendation with respect to quantum and geographical location of land to be acquired, safety aspects involved, if any and requirement of budget for the said acquisition. After that, General Manager (LRE) will forward the proposal with his recommendation to all the Functional Directors for their consent and finally produce the same to CMD, ECL for his approval. If any of the technical or financial parameter of the proposal crosses the limit provided in the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report then the said approval is to be obtained from the Board of Directors of ECL.

After obtaining approval of competent authority, Area General Manager will submit formal application along with required plans and documents to District Land Acquisition Authority for acquisition of specified quantum of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

He will authorize suitable executive (s) who will remain actively involved in the land acquisition activities, monitor progress of the same and intimate it to Area General Manager, keep liaison with the Land acquisition and State/ National monitoring authorities, attend the related meeting and put across views/ objections of ECL wherever necessary in those meetings. He will communicate Names, designations, places of posting and contact details of those executives to the Collector along with the application.

c Area General Manager will arrange to deposit the amount on account of payment of compensation and other R&R benefits as demanded by the Land Acquisition Authority under the provision of the Act. If demanded amount is beyond the limit of Budget provided to the Area then, Area Authority will take immediate step to collect additional budget required for the purpose from Head Quarter so that the demanded amount can be deposited to the District Authority within time stipulated in the Act.
