
#  PF-2

#  IMPORTANT STEPS IN ACQUISITION OF LAND THROUGH REGISTRATION OF SALE DEEDS UNDER INDIAN STAMP ACT, 1899

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual purchase of land)

##  A. FOR TENANCY LAND:

1. Approval is to be obtained from the Competent Authority regarding purchase of land for a mine following the procedure mentioned in Step 1.2.A and 1.2.B.

2. Landowner will submit application in Form-I showing intention to sell his lands to ECL (as per Step 2)

3. Lands offered by landowners in Form-I are to be compiled, thoroughly verified by Amin/RI/Surveyor of the mine and summary is to be prepared in Form-II (as per Step 3.2.A). Examination of all records in computerized form and in Hard copies is to be done to verify: that the offered plots –

i. Are included in the schedule of land already approved by the competent authority for the concerned Project/ mine;

ii. Do not belong to ECL by virtue of CMNA Act, 1973 or has not been acquired/ possessed earlier in any other mode of acquisition/purchase by the concerned colliery/ project or adjacent collieries/ areas after Nationalization of collieries,

iii. Is not included in 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan approved by the Central/ State Government for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government unless a specific approval of the competent authority is obtained in this regard (copy of the same is to be attached); and

iv. No compensation or benefit of any kind has been provided earlier against the scheduled land.

4. Legal opinion is to be obtained from empaneled Lawyer of ECL as per procedure mentioned in Step 3.2.B

5. Mine will initiate a proposal for approval of Area General Manager for purchasing the land scheduled in Form-II attaching all documents mentioned in Step 3.2.C. At the places in Jharkhand, where CNT Act is applicable, additional documents like draft copies of Affidavits, Agreements, Indemnity Bonds etc. as mentioned in Step 3.2.C are also to be attached for further examination and approval.

6. The proposal is to be examined and vetted by Area land Cell and then approved by the Area General Manager.

7. After above approval, summary of land (in Form-III) intended for purchasing is to be kept published, for at least three weeks, on official website of ECL inviting further information/ complaint from general mass with respect to the scheduled plots. Hard copy the publication is to be notified in the official Notice Board of the Unit/ Area and at conspicuous places of concerned villages and to be published in two widely circulated dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular (as per Step 3.2.D).

8. Area General Manager or a suitable Authority duly authorized by him for the purpose has to dispose off the complaints/ disputes within two weeks after expiry of three weeks of publication in website or receipt of complaint whichever is earlier (as per Step 3.2.E).

9. Final schedule of the land is to be suitably modified according to disposal of the complaints/ disputes to avoid purchase of any disputed land.

10. In West Bengal:

Land should be purchased by registration of Deed at concerned State Registration office from the person(s)/organization, whose name is recorded in the latest published Record of Right as landowner, as per the finally approved schedule.

i. Project Officer/ Agent of the Project/ Mine will sign on the Sale Deed on behalf of ECL. Purpose of purchase of land should be explicitly mentioned.

ii. The Deed should contain a schedule of land with details like, State, District, Police station, Mouza Name, Plot No, Khatian of landowner, Class/ type of land as per revenue record, total area of plot, share of landowner as per concerned Khatian (expressed in figures up to fourth decimal), Area of land being purchased in acres and total land being purchased through the Deed.

iii. A Part-Revenue-Plan showing concerned plots and geographical location of the areas being purchased with respect to each plot washed in specific color is to be attached to the Deed.

iv. If part of a plot is being purchased, where current vendor does not have legal title over rest part of the plot, then co-sharers should preferably sign as witnesses on the Deed and/or an Affidavit sworn by the co-sharers before a First Class Magistrate expressing their 'No-Objection' with respect to instant registration of sale deed is to be attached to the Deed and the same will become part of the deed by mentioning its reference in a Para within the Deed.

Or,

Following alternative measures may be observed in the situation of urgency for purchase of any part of a plot for the sake running a mine/ project but carrying out above measure under mission is not possible due to some valid and justified reasons:

A. Land should be purchased for specified share and share area as mentioned in the Record of Right of landowner as well as in the linked deed or linked ownership document.

B. It should be verified in the authenticated plot information that total of shares of all co-sharers is 1.0000, total of share areas of all co-sharers is equal to total area of the plot and share of the vendor matches with that mentioned in his RoR.

C. If total plot is to be procured as per approval, then action should be taken to ensure registration of deeds of different co-sharers takes place within short span of time.

D. An indemnity bond may be taken from the vendor where he shall indemnify all damages and losses of ECL including refund of consideration amount and other R&R benefits in the event of claim of possession by any other co-sharer over the land that he has sold to ECL by the specific registered sale deed or the sale deed is declared to be void or the vendor under the sale deed is declared to have no valid title by a court of law in future.

E. A clause may be inserted in the Deed as well as in the indemnity bond that, “the vendor has peaceful possession over the scheduled land. He hands over possession of the said land to Eastern Coalfields Limited for its business. If any other co-sharer claims possession over any of the scheduled plot in future then, he will return back land value and any other monetary compensation he has received in lieu of the scheduled land ECL will have full right to withdraw Rehabilitation & Resettlement benefit provided to him including termination of employment of the nominee of vendor of the scheduled land.”

##  In Jharkhand:

a) In some Districts of Jharkhand State CNT Act is in vogue. Here, registration of sale deed for Land transaction is possible only after obtaining permission of District Collector u/s 49 of the said Act.

b) At such places possession of land is taken by providing employment to landowners through Agreement. As per the prevalent Norm, the landowner has to affirm that the required permission of Collector will be submitted by him within two years of appointment of the nominated person otherwise his service may be terminated.

c) After obtaining approval from the Collector registration of sale deed is to be carried out abiding by procedure mentioned above for the State of West Bengal.

11. Land value is to be paid to the landowner(s) as per the negotiated rate approved for the project/scheme/ Underground working Panel.

12. Details of purchased land paid compensation are to be recorded and maintained by the Mine and Area Authorities in Form-D as per Step 3.2.F.

13. Physical Possession of the land is to be taken immediately after purchasing the land a document in this regard is to be prepared and preserved in Form- IV following procedure in Step 3.2.H.

14. The land is to be mutated in the revenue records of State Government following the procedure in Step 3.2.H.

15. Employment and other R&R Benefits will be provided following the procedure mentioned in Activity Steps 4,5,6,7 and 8.



#  B. Special Provision for purchase of debottar Land (land recorded in favour of deity):

As per the resolution passed in the meeting of Principal Secretary and Land revenue Commissioner on $26^{th}$ February,2020 and clarification given by the DLRS, West Bengal owner of Debottar land is a deity which is treated as perpetual minor by the law and custodian of the same is the District Judge. Some Orders have been passed in this regard by the District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman with respect to petitions filed by different Sebaits. In view of the said orders, another resolution was passed in the meeting held on $11^{th}$ January 2021. In the said resolution it has been stated that mutation of land has to be done referring the orders passed by the District Judge. For other cases, General Guideline would be issued later obtaining legal opinion of Ld. Advocate General. Subsequently, Deputy Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, Land & Land Reforms & RR & R Department, vide letter ref. no. DS-345/LA/3M-14/20 dated $08^{th}$ March, 2021, issued an Order stating,

"The matter was referred to the Ld. Legal Remembrancer, WB and after careful consideration of the matter and the orders of different courts mentioned in those letters it has been opined that no permission is required in case of alienation of private debuttore properties by sebait for legal necessity and for the benefit of the deity."

So, Area Authority has to get the land mutated in favour of ECL, after purchase of the land from recorded sebait, referring the aforesaid Order of the Deputy Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, Land & Land Reforms & RR & R Department.

Rest of the procedure is as described above for purchase of Tenancy land. All terms and conditions mentioned in the permission given by the District Judge, if any are to be strictly followed.

However, above procedure is subject to modification of policies and procedures circulated by the Government of West Bengal time to time.

##  C. Special Provision for purchase of Tribal Land:

As per the legal provision, Permission is to be obtained from the District Authority specified for this purpose. The tribal landowner has to apply to concerned District Authority attaching details and copies of documents with respect to the land he desires to sell. If he wants to nominate any non-tribal person for employment than same is to be specifically mentioned in the application for approval.

After obtaining above approval, procedure is to be followed as described above for purchase of Tenancy land.
