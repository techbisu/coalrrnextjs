

#  STEP 3: APPROVAL OF AREA GENERAL MANAGER BEFORE ACTUAL PURCHASE/ TAKING POSSESSION OF LAND AFTER SUBMISSION OF APPLICATION BY LAND OWNER

Activity 3.1: For land which has been acquired through CBA (A&D) Act-1957.

| A | R.O.Rs of the offered plot(s) are to be examined thoroughly and verified by the concerned Revenue Inspector / Amin / land clerk/ concerned colliery surveyor with the entries of 'Land purchase/ acquisition/ possession Register' and computerized land database to ensure that the offered plots were not purchased/ acquired/ possessed earlier. Based on the said examination and verification, a schedule of land offered by the landowner is to be prepared for purchase by ECL. This schedule of land is to be authenticated by the Unit Authorities as specified in the Form 'FORM - II'. in respect of its correctness based on the fact that - (i) The plots are included in the schedule of land already approved by the competent authority for the concerned Project/ mine (ii) The offered plots does not belong to ECL by virtue of CMNA Act, 1973 or has not been acquired/ possessed earlier in any other mode of acquisition by the concerned colliery/ project or adjacent collieries/ areas after Nationalization of collieries. (iii) Is not included in 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan approved by the Central/ State Government for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government unless a specific approval of the competent authority is obtained in this regard (copy of the same is to be attached); and (iv) No compensation or benefit of any kind has been provided earlier against the scheduled land. |
| A | An abstract of Form-II showing list of (a) legal heirs of Expired Landowners [who were owners on date notification u/s 9], (b) owners where minors rights are involved and legal procedure is required for providing R&R benefits and compensation payment, (c) Owners of lands of Deity which also may need legal procedures to settle cases, (c) subsequent purchasers where land has been transferred after the date of notification u/s 9. |
| B | Project Officer / Agent of the mine will requisition a certificate from an empanelled lawyer/ experienced advocate (who is practicing at Sub divisional or higher level of Court) attaching details of land and ownership documents and the affidavit(s) submitted by the landowners / persons interested' for issuance of a certificate by him. The concerned lawyer will carry out requisite searching in concerned land revenue and registration offices of the state Government regarding present status of ownership/ right to receive compensation and R&R benefits as a "Person interested' under the meaning of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 with respect to the above scheduled land and will issue certificate clearly indicating the following - (i) Whether title of the recorded landowner on the day of vesting of the land to the Central Government through Gazette notification under section 9 of the CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, as claimed, is genuine and authentic; (ii) If transfer of land has occurred through registration of sale deeds after the said notification u/s 9 of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 and the last transferee of the land has applied for handing over possession to ECL then whether transfer of right of 'person interested' has been properly occurred to the applicant from the recorded landowner (as on the day of vesting of the land the Central Government). (iii) Whether the applicant can get compensation and other R&R benefits from ECL on the basis of the documents of ownership and Affidavits submitted by him and the report of searching. (ii) Whether the land is free from any encumbrance, disputes, legal obligation which bar/ restrict the right of the claimant to receive compensation and R&R benefits. |

| C | After this, a proposal is to be initiated by the Unit Management mentioning details and abstract of the land to be possessed and purpose of same attaching application of the claimant (FORM-I) along with related documents submitted by the claimant, duly signed prescribed Proforma ('FORM-II'), copy of a revenue plan showing the scheduled land and year wise phasing of planned use of the land, copies of approval of Competent Authority obtained earlier for possession of the land, information regarding duration of earlier website publication along with copies of relevant pages of Form III which were uploaded in official website of the company, copies of publication in two dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular (as per step 1.1.D) and all other relevant documents as may be deemed necessary for obtaining approval of concerned General Manager of the Area for taking physical possession of the scheduled land and initiation of formal proposal for offering employment/ other R&R benefits as per the provisions stipulated in concerned statute and/ or prevalent R&R policy of CIL.Before placing the proposal for approval of concerned General Manager it should be verified and vetted by the Area Land Cell Committee so as to ensure that the scheduled plots of land are neither included in land inventory of the company by virtue of inheritance under CMNA, 1973 or previous acquisition through any other mode nor included in the schedule of land for 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government. |
| D | If, for any reason whatsoever, the land scheduled for the current proposal was not published in the official website of the company earlier in Step 1.1.D then, after approval of the proposal by the concerned Area General Manager the schedule of land is to be Published in official website of ECL showing intension of the company to possess the land from the landowners mentioned therein (in FORM - III) and inviting further information regarding any dispute or complain with respect to the instant plots from general mass that can be submitted in person or through postal communication in the office of the General Manager of the area/ office of the Agent/ Project Officer or in the official Grievance-Redressal web portal within three weeks of publication in the website. Hard copy of the publication is to be displayed in the official Notice Board of the Unit/ Area and at conspicuous places of concerned villages and to be published in two widely circulated dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular. |
| E | Complaints, if any, is to be disposed off through required investigation and/ or hearing etc within next two weeks by the concerned General Manager or a suitable Authority duly authorized by him for the purpose. The schedule of the land may be suitably corrected and approved by the concerned General Manager after disposal of the complaints and deleting lands already owned by ECL/ under dispute of ownership/ subjudice at tribunal or court of law/ transfer is restricted by any provision of statute/ not required any more due to any reason whatsoever. Physical possession of Land should be taken as per the finally approved schedule of the land providing compensation and benefits as mentioned in the Award.Area General Manager should also accord administrative approval for deposition of compensation amount to the tribunal for disputed/ sub-judice lands and those lands for which no claims are submitted by the landowners. Physical possession of such land should be taken after deposition of awarded compensation amount at the concerned tribunal. Area land dealing officer or any other executive of the Area nominated by Area General Manager for the purpose will be responsible for depositing the compensation amount within three months of obtaining above approval from the Area General Manager. |
| F | Physical possession of land is to be taken by the Unit/Area Authority on the field. Certificates of taking physical possession will be duly signed by the 'persons interest', nominated Unit Authority of ECL and two witnesses preferably members of the Village Land Committee/ village rehabilitation committee etc in prescribed from (FORM-IV). Possessed land should be cleared by dozing/ trenching at the boundary etc. by mechanical means. Area Land dealing officer will ensure the same within seven days taking possession and will report it to the General Manager of the Area in Form-IV (Part-B). |

| G | After possession of the land, details of the said land is to be recorded within three days in the bound paged Register ('Land purchase/ acquisition/ possession Register') kept at Unit Office for the purpose and the 'Computerized Land inventory record' maintained in prescribed format and/ or any other computer based data and information land inventory recording system that may be devised and implemented subsequently is to be updated accordingly. Every such entry in the said register will be countersigned by the Unit Survey/ RI/ Amin/any official authorized for this purpose by the Agent/ Project Officer, Manager and Agent/ Project officer mentioning full name, designation, date along with official seal. A copy of the said 'Land purchase/ acquisition/ possession Register' and computerized land record is to be maintained by the Area Land department for all the colliers under its command. It will be the duty of the Agent/ Project officer of the mine to communicate details of land taken in possession to the concerned Area land dealing officer within three days and Area Land Dealing officer will update records in said register and computerized land inventory database within seven days. Every such entry in the said register will be countersigned by the concerned RI/ Amin/ Official authorized to maintain land record and Area Land dealing Officer mentioning full name, designation, date along with official seal. |

| C | After this, a proposal is to be initiated by the Unit mentioning details and abstract of the land to be acquired/ purchased and purpose of same thereof attaching application of the claim (Form-I) along with related documents submitted by the claimant, duly signed prescribed Proforma ('Form-II'), copy of a revenue plan showing the scheduled land and year wise phasing of planned use of the land, Copies of relevant pages of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report which depicts provisions of land requirement and cost of land acquisition for the project/ opencast patch/ underground working panel, Copy of land use plan enclosed in approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report, if any, copies of approval of Competent Authority obtained earlier for purchase of the land, approval of concerned authority for purchase of tribal or debottar land and all other relevant documents as may be deemed necessary for obtaining approval of concerned General Manager of the Area for purchase of the scheduled land.In the State of Jarkhand where CNT Act is applicable, ownership of the land should be determined on the basis of Concerned Circle Officer's ownership/possession Certificate and certification that the name of applicant appears as landowner in Register-II, approval of District Authority, associated linked deeds, if any. If the proposal has been initiated for taking possession of land through Agreement with landowner then, a copy of the prior approval of Competent Authority obtained for the purpose is to be attached and copies of draft (unsigned) Affidavit, Agreement and Indemnity Bond depicting details of land, employment provisions and other terms and conditions are to be attached to the proposal for approval. In the said Agreement, following clause must be inserted,"It would be the responsibility of the landowners to hand over the copy of the approval of State Authority in respect of transfer of land, which is involved in employment, to ECL within next two years. If ECL does not receive the said approval due to the fact that either the ownership of the landowner over the land is not authenticated by the State Authority or the Competent Authority of State Government denies to approve the application for transfer of the land in favour of ECL for any other reason whatsoever then, ECL may take any disciplinary action including termination of service of the nominee who has been employed against the concerned land."Before placing the proposal for approval of concerned General Manager it should be verified and vetted by the Area Land Cell Committee so as to ensure that the scheduled plots of land are neither included in land inventory of the company by virtue of inheritance under CMNA, 1973 or previous acquisition through any other mode nor included in the schedule of land for 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government. The committee will also certify that no compensation and R&R benefit was provided earlier against the said land and whether employment claim will be admissible afterwards based on the date of use of the land. |

| E | Land should be purchased by registration of Deed at concerned State Registration Office from the person(s)/organization, whose name is recorded in the latest published Record of Right as landowner, as per the finally approved schedule of the land only and Land compensation admissible to the landowner is to be paid. Project Officer/ Agent of the Project/ Mine will sign on the Sale Deed on behalf of ECL. Purpose of purchase of land should be explicitly mentioned. The Deed should contain a schedule of land with details like, State, District, Police station, Mouza Name, Plot No., khatiyan of landowner, Class/ type of land as per revenue record, total area of plot, share of landowner as per concerned khatiyan (expressed in figures up to fourth decimal), Area of land being purchased in acres (expressed in figures up to fourth decimal) and total land being purchased through the Deed. A part revenue plan showing concerned plots and geographical location of the areas being purchased with respect to each plot washed in specific colour is to be attached to the Deed. |
| F | After purchase of the land, details of the said land is to be recorded within three days in the bound paged Register ('Land purchase/ acquisition/ possession Register') kept at Unit Office for the purpose and the 'Computerized Land Inventory Record' maintained in prescribed format and/ or any other computer based data and information land inventory recording system that may be devised and implemented subsequently is to be updated accordingly. Details of compensation (Land compensation and 'One Time Monetary Compensation in lieu of employment', if any) is to be entered in 'Form-D' and kept recorded in the bound paged 'COMPENSATION AND R&R REGISTER' as well in computer in the same form. Every such entry in the said registers will be countersigned by the Unit Surveyor/ RI/ Amin/any official authorized for this purpose by the Agent/ Project Officer, Manager and Agent/ Project officer mentioning full name, designation, date along with official seal. A copy of the said 'Land purchase/ acquisition/ possession Register', 'COMPENSATION AND R&R REGISTER' and computerized land record is to be maintained by the Area Land department for all the colonies under its command. It will be the duty of the Agent/ Project officer of the mine to communicate details of land purchased and taken in possession and compensation paid to the concerned Area land dealing officer within three days and Area Land Dealing officer will update records in said registers and computerized land inventory database within seven days. Every such entry in the said register will be countersigned by the concerned RI/Amin/ Officers authorized to maintain land record and Area Land dealing Officer mentioning full name, designation, date along with official seal mentioning full name, designation, date along with official seal. |
| G | Handing over of physical possession of the land should be carried out in the field immediately after purchase of the same in front of two witnesses preferably members of the Village Land Committee/ village rehabilitation committee etc. A written document of the same is to be prepared and executed under signature of transferrer of the land, representative of Area/ Unit, and the said witnesses (FORM- IV). in case of taking possession of land through agreement all the approved Affidavit, Agreement and Indemnity Bond are to be executed and the terms and conditions mentioned therein are to be strictly followed. |
| H | Application for mutation in favour of ECL is to be submitted to the concerned State revenue Office immediately after purchase of land. Application for claim of employment involving the land cannot be processed until the same is mutated in favour of the company. |
