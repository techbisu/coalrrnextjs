
#  PF-3

##  IMPORTANT STEPS IN TRANSFER OF GOVERNMENT LAND AND 'PATTA-LAND' THROUGH ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual submission of application for transfer of land. Process-Flow described below is being followed in the State of West Bengal only. State of Jharkhand has separate lease policy for transfer of Government land to Central Government Companies at the time of preparation of the SOP. Procedure as circulated and practiced by the concerned State Government should be followed)

##  A. For transfer of encumbrance-free Government Land

A. Proposal is submitted by the General Manager of the concerned Area at District Land Acquisition office requesting transfer of government land attaching:

I. Details of the mine/ project for which the land is required;

II. Copy of relevant pages approved PR/ Scheme of the mine which shows requirement of the land;

III. Details of the required Government land;

IV. Plan showing location of the propose land with respect to the mine/ project.

B. Area State Revenue department and ADSR office separately calculate value of the land. Higher of the two is considered and Transfer value is determined by the District Authority.

C. Then, the proposal is sent by the Collector with recommendation for lease/ perpetual transfer of the land, as the case may be, to State Headquarter for approval.

D. After approval of the proposal by the State Government, District Land Acquisition Authority raise demand.

E. After obtaining demand from the District Authority, Area General Manager has to pay the demanded amount from budget available in the Area under the specific head for the particular project/ mine. In case of insufficiency of fund in the Area the same is to be obtained from corporate level by initiating specific proposal for the purpose attaching all relevant documents like copy of Scheme/ Project Report, Plan of the project showing location of the land, demand of the State Government, documents related to land, copy of approval of the land, copy of approved schedule of land etc.

F. If the demanded amount surpasses the provision of Capital provided earlier then fresh approval is to be obtained for payment of the demanded amount from the same Authority who accorded earlier approval.

G. After payment of the demanded of amount, District Land acquisition Authority hands over possession of land to Area Authority. Representatives of District land acquisition Authority and Area/ Project Authority signs on the said possession document.

H. A deed of conveyance in specified format supplied by the District authority is signed by the representatives of both State Government and ECL and is registered for transfer of land.

I. Mutation of land is done in favour of ECL.

##  B. For Patta Land that has not been acquired under Coal Bearing Areas (Acquisition and Development) Act 1957, The Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 or any other State land acquisition Act:

ECL Board has approved following modus operandi in its $296^{th}$ meeting for acquisition of Government land on which Patta has been issued by the State Government:



1. The Patta holder will submit application claiming employment and/or other R&R benefits furnishing details of land being offered for the claim and attaching copies of Patta certificate, ROR and other ownership documents, if any, to concerned Mine Authority requesting acquisition of the land under his possession.

2. Area Authority and the Patta holder will sign on an Agreement [as per the standard draft of the agreement approved in $296^{th}$ Meeting of ECL Board (Form XX)] that, Patta holder will get employment and/or other R&R benefits admissible to him with the conditions that –

i. Patta holder will voluntarily apply to the concerned SDO for cancellation/annulment of the Patta;

ii. Patta holder will relinquish his Patta right and will handover encumbrance-free possession of the land to the State Government;

iii. If Patta holder has less than 2.00 acres of land then, he will be able to club together his land with land of other landowners within the chunk of land wherein his land lies and which has been approved for purchase along with employment under package deal concept to the extent of two acres and nominate one of the land losers among the groups or their dependent for employment;

iv. ECL Will apply to the concerned District Authority for transfer of the land as soon as possible after cancellation of Patta;

v. His application for employment will be processed for approval after cancellation of his Patta. But his appointment will be released only after handing over possession of the concerned Govt. on which Patta was issued earlier to him land by concerned State Govt. authority to ECL.

3. Patta holder will also submit the checklist no. CL-4 and documents as per the said checklist including an Affidavit [as per the standard draft of the affidavit approved in $296^{th}$ Meeting of ECL Board(Form XIX)] sworn before first class executive Magistrate stating name of the nominee of employment, total schedule of land for which employment has been claimed, genuiness of copies of all the documents submitted along with his claim of employment and his acceptance of the conditions mentioned in the aforesaid agreement.

4. Patta holder will submit application attaching copies of Patta Documents and Record of Rights to the Sub-Divisional Officer for cancelation of his Patta.

5. After observing the procedure laid down in the statute and guideline issued by the State Government SDO will annul/ cancel the Patta.

6. After obtaining copy of the cancelation/annulment of the Patta, Area General Manager will submit application for transfer of the government land on which Patta has been cancelled/annulled. All the steps mentioned above for transfer of encumbrance-free Government land will be executed for transfer of the land.

7. Simultaneously, the Patta Holder may apply (in Form-I) attaching all relevant documents specified in ‘Activity Step 2’ and copy of the cancelation certificate issued by the SDO.

8. The employment proposal will be examined subject to R&R Policy and prevalent norms of the company. It will be processed through Area Land cell committee as per normal procedure and should be specifically recommended by the General Manager of the Area. Procedures depicted under Steps 4,5,6 and 7 will followed for approval of the employment.

9. Approved employment proposal will be forwarded to General Manager (P&IR), ECL for further examination like verification of testimonials, examination of medical fitness, police verification etc. as done for other employment proposals against land; but, appointment letter will be released only after obtaining certificate from the General Manager of concerned Area that ECL has received possession from the State Government authority with respect to the Government land, on which Patta was issued earlier to the concerned applicant.
