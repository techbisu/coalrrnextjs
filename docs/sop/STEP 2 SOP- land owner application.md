

#  STEP 2: SUBMISSION OF APPLICATION BY THE LANDOWNER

##  Activity 1.0:

Application of the Tenant (s) /Rayat (s) to Unit Authority/ Area Authority giving his/ her consent for selling/ handing over possession of land to ECL Associated Form: FORM - I (Application for transfer of land) along with copies of required supporting documents.

##  Eligibility:

a) For application of outright purchase - Landowner/ 'Sebayet of private debottar land' whose name is recorded in the last published ROR by the State Revenue Department or whose name appears as landowner/ 'Sebayet of private debottar land' in the official tenant's register/ database maintained by the State Revenue department;

b) For application of possession of land already acquired by ECL under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 -

i) A person whose name apparars as landowner/ 'sebayet of private debottar land' in the official tenant's register/ database maintained by the State Revenue department as on the date of Notification in the Gazette of of India u/s 9 of the CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 or if the said recorded landowner/'sebayet of private debottar land' is not alive then his legal heirs; or

ii) If transfer of land has occurred through registration of sale deeds after the said notification u/s 9 of CBA (A&D) Act 1957, recorded landowner as on the date of Notification in the Gazette of India u/s 9 of the CBA (A&D) Act 1957 of said notification has declared through written Affidavit sworn in front of First Class Magistrate to relinquish his rights of being 'person interested' and transfer the same rights to the last transferee of the said land and Competent Authority has approved the same then, the last transferee of the land;
