##  PROCESS FLOW FOR ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual acquisition of land)

1. Schedule of the land and graphical representation of the same on Revenue/ Mouza map are to be prepared for acquisition under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957. Total quantum of land must be within approved limit mentioned in Project Report/ Scheme (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

2. Above schedule is to be verified with computerized and physical records of the concerned collieries to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

3. Above schedule is to be reconciled with land records of neighboring mines/ Areas in 'FORM-VII' to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

4. Proposal is initiated by the mine management (signed by Surveyor, land dealing official, safety officer, Project officer/ Agent) and will be forwarded to the General Manager of the concerned Area attaching

i. Copy of approval of the Project Report/ Scheme accorded by competent authority;

ii. Copy of relevant pages of the Project Report/ Scheme (if available or reason for non-availability),

iii. Copy of Geological report,

iv. schedule of Mouza-wise land areas to be acquired,

v. Five copies of Mouza maps showing specified areas in different mouzas that has been proposed for acquisition,

vi. statements and certificates in Form 1A, 1B, 1C and 1D and

vii. Any other document which will be needed for notification under Section 4 of the Act,

viii. Soft copies of all above documents.

for obtaining competent approval with respect to –

a. Acquisition of scheduled land under the said Act.

b. Processing and submitting application to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India for Notification under section 4, 7, 9 and 11 of the Act.

c. For publishing intention of acquisition of scheduled land in company website and in two dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular once after Notification under section 7 and once after Notification under section 11.

d. For carrying out socio-economic survey by a committee/ expert agency within two months of Notification under section 7 for identification of PAFs/ displaced families and recording baseline information and data regarding the same.

e. Capturing satellite image of the total acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the proposed area of acquisition immediately after Notification u/s 9.

5. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by the Area Land Cell and Area General Manager. Then it should be forwarded to General Manager (LRE), Headquarter.

6. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the General Manager (Project & Planning), General Manager (Safety) and General Manager (Finance) In-charge. Then the proposal is to be forwarded for consent of Director (Technical) Project & Planning, Director (Technical) Operation, Director (Finance) and approval of CMD, ECL

7. After obtaining competent approval of the proposal by competent authority, online Application will be uploaded in the specified Central Government Portal by General Manager (LRE) for publication of



Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 4 of the Act. Hard copy of the same may also be forwarded to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India, if needed, as per existing guideline/ practice for the said purpose.

8. After publication of Notification under section 4 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Submit all documents like, detailed schedule of plots for acquisition, Copy of relevant pages of the Project Report/ Scheme, Copy of Geological report, Five copies of Mouza maps showing specified areas in different mouzas that has been proposed for acquisition statements and certificates in prescribed Form 2A, 2B, 2C, 2D etc. and Any other document which will be needed for notification under Section 7 of the Act. (Care should be taken to complete this step as early as possible because maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year).

II. Will entrust suitable officials of his Area for collection of data and information regarding ownership of the lands and getting the same authenticated by the concerned authorities of District revenue department.

9. General Manager (LRE) will arrange to submit online Application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in ‘Gazette of India’ u/s 7 of the Act.

10. After publication of Notification under section 7 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Submit all documents required for Notification under section 9 to General Manager (LRE).

II. Publish the content of Notification u/s 7 along with plot-schedule of land in website of the company and in two newspapers, one in well circulated English daily and one in well circulated local vernacular.

III. Form a committee involving Collector of the District or appoint an expert agency for carrying out socio-economic survey for identification of PAFs/ displaced families and recording baseline information and data regarding the same. The said survey should be completed within two months of Notification under section 7.

IV. Send one original copy and one photocopy of the above newspaper publications to General Manager (LRE).

11. General Manager (LRE) will forward application along with photocopy of Notification u/s 7 and original copy of the newspaper publications to the Competent Authority (Coal Controller or any other Authority as notified by the Central Government) for issuance of report u/s 8.

12. General Manager (LRE) will submit online application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 9 of the Act. He will also forward a copy of the report of 'Competent Authority' u/s 8 of the Act to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India (If the said Authority does not send the report directly to Ministry of Coal).

13. After publication of Notification under section 9 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. submit all documents required for Notification under section 11 to General Manager (LRE).

II. Send written request to General Manager (P&P) and General Manager (Systems) attaching required plan and other information like latitude/ longitude of the acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the area of acquisition for capturing Satellite image of the same area.

14. General Manager (P&P) with the help of General Manager (Systems) will arrange to capture Satellite image, as requested, immediately and send copies of the said image to Area General Manager and to General Manager (LRE).

15. If Rehabilitation of PAFs are involved then Area General Manager will take immediate actions as per Activity Step- 8 of the SOP for conducting census and socio-economic study for collection of baseline data and issuance of identity cards to the ‘Displaced Families.’



16. General Manager (LRE) will arrange to submit online Application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 11 of the Act.

17. After publication of Notification under section 11 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Publish the content of Notification u/s 11 along with plot-schedule of land as notified u/s 9 in website of the company and in two newspapers, one in well circulated English daily and one in well circulated local vernacular.

II. Paste copies of Notification u/s 11 along with plot-schedule of land as notified u/s 9 at Panchayet Bhawnas and at other conspicuous places in the mouzas where acquisition has taken place.

III. Proclaim the same in and nearby areas/villages by beat of drum.

IV. If Rehabilitation of PAFs are involved then he will take immediate actions as per approved 'Rehabilitation Planning Guideline' or in absence of the same for preparation and consent with respect to 'Scheme for Rehabilitation' as described in Activity Step- 8 of the SOP.

V. For Rehabilitation of PAFs Area General Manager will also take steps for constitution of Resettlement & Rehabilitation Committee as described in Activity Step- 8.B.6 of the SOP.

18. Landowner will submit application in Form-I claiming compensation and R&R benefits along with all the documents mentioned in Activity Step 2.

19. Simultaneously, A formal proposal is to be initiated by the Mine/ project for taking physical possession of land which has been acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 with all the documents and plans mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.B.

20. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by Area Land Cell as well as Area General Manager. (Step 1.1.C)

21. Abstract of land (in Form-III) intended for purchasing is to be kept published, for at least three weeks, on official website of ECL inviting further information/ complaint from general mass with respect to the scheduled plots. Hard copy the publication is to be notified in the official Notice Board of the Unit/ Area and at conspicuous places of concerned villages and to be published in two widely circulated dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular (as per Activity Step 1.1.D).

22. Area General Manager or a suitable Authority duly authorized by him for the purpose has to dispose off the complaints/ disputes within two weeks after expiry of three weeks of publication in website or receipt of complaint whichever is earlier (as per Step 1.1.E). He will forward the proposal to GM (LRE) with specific recommendation and modified schedule after resolution of disputes. List of the disputed land may also be attached, if possible, for which compensation will have to be deposited at tribunal (as per Activity Step 1.1.E).

23. GM (LRE) will forward the proposal to GM (Project & Planning) and GM (Fin) I/C. They will examine the proposal in light of approved project parameters, year-wise production plan and financial criteria of the project respectively and will recommend accordingly (as per Activity Step 1.1.F).

24. After this, GM (LRE) will forward the proposal for consent of concerned Director (Tech), Director (Personnel) and Director (Fin) with his specific recommendation. Then, the same will be forwarded to CMD, ECL for approval if the number of employment and other parameters are within the provisions of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report. If the proposed parameters exceed the provisions of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report then the proposal is to be placed in meeting of Board of Directors of ECL for approval (as per Activity Step 1.1.G).

25. A Compensation Payroll (Details in Standard Form 1A and Abstract in standard Form 1B) for the concerned landowners is to be prepared by the unit under section 13(5) or section 14(1) of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 as the case may be. For Compensation Payroll prepared under Section 13(5) of the Act Government notified market value prevalent on the date of determination of compensation is to be considered. Market value is to be determined as per the provision of Section 26 of the RFCTLARR Act,



2013. The Compensation Payroll with a formal proposal will be forwarded to the Area General Manager attaching information/ documents mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.H.

26. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by Area Land Cell as well as Area General Manager and will be forwarded to GM (LRE). Conditions mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.I are to be followed. Statutory provisions and related clarifications issued by the Central/ State Government are to be obeyed strictly.

27. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the GM (Project & Planning) and to General Manager (Finance) and forward the same with specific recommendation to the CMD, ECL for approval after obtaining consent for the same from concerned Director (Tech) and Director (Fin) (as per Activity Step 1.1.J).

28. General Manager (LRE) will forward the approved compensation payroll to the Area General Manager for publication of the award u/s 13(5) or 14(1) in the public domain of official website of ECL and pasting the same at conspicuous places of the concerned villages asking the landowners to handover physical possession of land receiving awarded compensation and other allowances and submission of application for employment if he is entitled to. Every such published Award must have a unique Number and Date (as per Activity Step 1.1.K).

29. It shall be mentioned in the published Award that, all the notified landowners have to claim the compensation amount submitting bank account details for RTGS along with self-signed Form-I and associated documents and that, it may be deposited in the tribunal if they fail to do so within two months of publication of the Award in official website of ECL.

30. Landowners/ affected families may submit application along with all documents as per Activity Step 2.

31. Area Authority will start disbursement of awarded compensation amount and provide approved R&R benefits to the concerned landowner/ persons interested/ affected families based on their submitted claims and documents, entitlements as per the provisions of Statute and approvals obtained in this regard. Procedure described in Activity Step 1.1.M and 3.1 is to be followed.

32. Physical possession of Land should be taken as per the finally approved schedule of the land providing compensation and benefits as mentioned in the Award.

33. Area General Manager should also accord administrative approval for deposition of compensation amount to the tribunal for disputed/ sub-judice lands and those lands for which no claims are submitted by the landowners. Physical possession of such land should be taken after deposition of awarded compensation amount at the concerned tribunal. Area land dealing officer or any other executive of the Area nominated by Area General Manager for the purpose will be responsible for depositing the compensation amount within three months of obtaining above approval from the Area General Manager.

34. All the information with respect to compensation paid and R&R Benefits provided must be entered in FORM D and kept recorded in a bound paged book ('COMPENSATION AND R&R REGISTER'). The same is also to be preserved in computer in the same Format. Entries of the said register are to be signed by colliery land dealing official, manager and finance executive. Manager will communicate the said entries to the Area Land dealing officer. Area Land dealing officer will maintain copy of the same register and will sign on it (as per Activity Step 1.1.N).

35. Employment and other R&R Benefits will be provided following the procedure mentioned in Activity Steps 4,5,6, and 7.

#  IMPORTANT SECTS OF CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

Notification u/s 4(1): Publication of preliminary notification for prospecting purpose

Notification u/s 7(1): Notice showing Intention of the Central Government to acquire part or whole area of the land notified u/s 4 for coal mining. Plot-wise details are provided in the notification. Maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year.

Notification u/s 8(1): Any 'person interested' may lodge a complaint over any portion of land notified u/s 7(1) within thirty days of the said Notification. Notified 'Competent Authority' (Coal Controller at present). Will dispose off the complaints after hearing and his specific recommendation for decision of the Central Government

Notice u/s 12: Power of the competent authority (notified u/s 3) to take possession of the land. He will give notice to the landowner/ ‘person interested’ to hand over possession of the land within such period as he deems fit. In case of any hindrance law and order machinery may be used.



Section 13(5): compensation to be paid to the 'person interested' based on the market value and other damages in the law.

As per Guideline of Central Government: Compensation amount u/s 13 should consist of (a) Market value of land as on the date of notification u/s 4, (b) Solatium @ 30% on the market value of land (c) Interest amount   12% for the period between notification u/s 4 and 9 (Max. three years) +   9% for one year after notification u/s 9 and @ 15% then after till payment is made

Section 14(1): Payment of compensation may be made as per agreed rate. If no agreement could be reached, the matter may be resolved by award given by a Tribunal specially formed for the purpose (For ECL it is at Godda Jharkhand)

Section 17: Central Government has to pay compensation to the 'person interested'. By Gazette Notification, S.O. 2378 dated $13^{th}$ October 2017 Central Government has delegated this power to CIL and its subsidiaries

#  IMPORTANT GAZETTE NOTIFICATIONS AND CLARIFICATIONS OF CENTRAL GOVERNMENT:

Gazette Notification 2368(E) Dated 28th August 2015 [RFCTLARR (Removal of Difficulty Order)]:Central Government has Notified that Compensation and R&R benefits for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 has to be provided as per First, Second and Third Schedule of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.This is effected from 1st September 2015.

As per clarification provided by MoC (dated $4^{th}$ August 2017, $30^{th}$ March 2018 and $28^{th}$ June 2019):

1. General Manager of the concerned Area, being Competent Authority as per notification u/s 3 of RFCTLARR Act, 2013, is the competent Authority for determination of compensation for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957.

2. If determination of compensation has been done before the cut-off date ($1^{st}$ September 2015) then the same cannot be reopened irrespective of whether possession has been taken or not.

3. Where prior to 01.09.2015, declaration of acquisition has been published under CBA Act, but determination of compensation has not been made under Sec. 13(5) of the CBA Act, compensation will be determined following the provisions of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013 irrespective of whether possession has been taken or not

4. Fresh agreement with ‘affected family’ u/s 14(1) may be executed if it is more beneficial to him.

#  IMPORTANT STEPS IN ACQUISITION OF LAND THROUGH REGISTRATION OF SALE DEEDS UNDER INDIAN STAMP ACT, 1899

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual purchase of land)

##  A. FOR TENANCY LAND:

1. Approval is to be obtained from the Competent Authority regarding purchase of land for a mine following the procedure mentioned in Step 1.2.A and 1.2.B.

2. Landowner will submit application in Form-I showing intention to sell his lands to ECL (as per Step 2)

3. Lands offered by landowners in Form-I are to be compiled, thoroughly verified by Amin/RI/Surveyor of the mine and summary is to be prepared in Form-II (as per Step 3.2.A). Examination of all records in computerized form and in Hard copies is to be done to verify: that the offered plots –

i. Are included in the schedule of land already approved by the competent authority for the concerned Project/ mine;

ii. Do not belong to ECL by virtue of CMNA Act, 1973 or has not been acquired/ possessed earlier in any other mode of acquisition/purchase by the concerned colliery/ project or adjacent collieries/ areas after Nationalization of collieries,

iii. Is not included in 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan approved by the Central/ State Government for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government unless a specific approval of the competent authority is obtained in this regard (copy of the same is to be attached); and

iv. No compensation or benefit of any kind has been provided earlier against the scheduled land.

4. Legal opinion is to be obtained from empaneled Lawyer of ECL as per procedure mentioned in Step 3.2.B

5. Mine will initiate a proposal for approval of Area General Manager for purchasing the land scheduled in Form-II attaching all documents mentioned in Step 3.2.C. At the places in Jharkhand, where CNT Act is applicable, additional documents like draft copies of Affidavits, Agreements, Indemnity Bonds etc. as mentioned in Step 3.2.C are also to be attached for further examination and approval.

6. The proposal is to be examined and vetted by Area land Cell and then approved by the Area General Manager.

7. After above approval, summary of land (in Form-III) intended for purchasing is to be kept published, for at least three weeks, on official website of ECL inviting further information/ complaint from general mass with respect to the scheduled plots. Hard copy the publication is to be notified in the official Notice Board of the Unit/ Area and at conspicuous places of concerned villages and to be published in two widely circulated dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular (as per Step 3.2.D).

8. Area General Manager or a suitable Authority duly authorized by him for the purpose has to dispose off the complaints/ disputes within two weeks after expiry of three weeks of publication in website or receipt of complaint whichever is earlier (as per Step 3.2.E).

9. Final schedule of the land is to be suitably modified according to disposal of the complaints/ disputes to avoid purchase of any disputed land.

10. In West Bengal:

Land should be purchased by registration of Deed at concerned State Registration office from the person(s)/organization, whose name is recorded in the latest published Record of Right as landowner, as per the finally approved schedule.

i. Project Officer/ Agent of the Project/ Mine will sign on the Sale Deed on behalf of ECL. Purpose of purchase of land should be explicitly mentioned.


#  SOP-PART B

ii. The Deed should contain a schedule of land with details like, State, District, Police station, Mouza Name, Plot No, Khatian of landowner, Class/ type of land as per revenue record, total area of plot, share of landowner as per concerned Khatian (expressed in figures up to fourth decimal), Area of land being purchased in acres and total land being purchased through the Deed.

iii. A Part-Revenue-Plan showing concerned plots and geographical location of the areas being purchased with respect to each plot washed in specific color is to be attached to the Deed.

iv. If part of a plot is being purchased, where current vendor does not have legal title over rest part of the plot, then co-sharers should preferably sign as witnesses on the Deed and/or an Affidavit sworn by the co-sharers before a First Class Magistrate expressing their 'No-Objection' with respect to instant registration of sale deed is to be attached to the Deed and the same will become part of the deed by mentioning its reference in a Para within the Deed.

Or,

Following alternative measures may be observed in the situation of urgency for purchase of any part of a plot for the sake running a mine/ project but carrying out above measure under mission is not possible due to some valid and justified reasons:

A. Land should be purchased for specified share and share area as mentioned in the Record of Right of landowner as well as in the linked deed or linked ownership document.

B. It should be verified in the authenticated plot information that total of shares of all co-sharers is 1.0000, total of share areas of all co-sharers is equal to total area of the plot and share of the vendor matches with that mentioned in his RoR.

C. If total plot is to be procured as per approval, then action should be taken to ensure registration of deeds of different co-sharers takes place within short span of time.

D. An indemnity bond may be taken from the vendor where he shall indemnify all damages and losses of ECL including refund of consideration amount and other R&R benefits in the event of claim of possession by any other co-sharer over the land that he has sold to ECL by the specific registered sale deed or the sale deed is declared to be void or the vendor under the sale deed is declared to have no valid title by a court of law in future.

E. A clause may be inserted in the Deed as well as in the indemnity bond that, “the vendor has peaceful possession over the scheduled land. He hands over possession of the said land to Eastern Coalfields Limited for its business. If any other co-sharer claims possession over any of the scheduled plot in future then, he will return back land value and any other monetary compensation he has received in lieu of the scheduled land ECL will have full right to withdraw Rehabilitation & Resettlement benefit provided to him including termination of employment of the nominee of vendor of the scheduled land.”

##  In Jharkhand:

a) In some Districts of Jharkhand State CNT Act is in vogue. Here, registration of sale deed for Land transaction is possible only after obtaining permission of District Collector u/s 49 of the said Act.

b) At such places possession of land is taken by providing employment to landowners through Agreement. As per the prevalent Norm, the landowner has to affirm that the required permission of Collector will be submitted by him within two years of appointment of the nominated person otherwise his service may be terminated.

c) After obtaining approval from the Collector registration of sale deed is to be carried out abiding by procedure mentioned above for the State of West Bengal.

11. Land value is to be paid to the landowner(s) as per the negotiated rate approved for the project/scheme/ Underground working Panel.

12. Details of purchased land paid compensation are to be recorded and maintained by the Mine and Area Authorities in Form-D as per Step 3.2.F.

13. Physical Possession of the land is to be taken immediately after purchasing the land a document in this regard is to be prepared and preserved in Form- IV following procedure in Step 3.2.H.

14. The land is to be mutated in the revenue records of State Government following the procedure in Step 3.2.H.

15. Employment and other R&R Benefits will be provided following the procedure mentioned in Activity Steps 4,5,6,7 and 8.



#  B. Special Provision for purchase of debottar Land (land recorded in favour of deity):

As per the resolution passed in the meeting of Principal Secretary and Land revenue Commissioner on $26^{th}$ February,2020 and clarification given by the DLRS, West Bengal owner of Debottar land is a deity which is treated as perpetual minor by the law and custodian of the same is the District Judge. Some Orders have been passed in this regard by the District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman with respect to petitions filed by different Sebaits. In view of the said orders, another resolution was passed in the meeting held on $11^{th}$ January 2021. In the said resolution it has been stated that mutation of land has to be done referring the orders passed by the District Judge. For other cases, General Guideline would be issued later obtaining legal opinion of Ld. Advocate General. Subsequently, Deputy Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, Land & Land Reforms & RR & R Department, vide letter ref. no. DS-345/LA/3M-14/20 dated $08^{th}$ March, 2021, issued an Order stating,

"The matter was referred to the Ld. Legal Remembrancer, WB and after careful consideration of the matter and the orders of different courts mentioned in those letters it has been opined that no permission is required in case of alienation of private debuttore properties by sebait for legal necessity and for the benefit of the deity."

So, Area Authority has to get the land mutated in favour of ECL, after purchase of the land from recorded sebait, referring the aforesaid Order of the Deputy Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, Land & Land Reforms & RR & R Department.

Rest of the procedure is as described above for purchase of Tenancy land. All terms and conditions mentioned in the permission given by the District Judge, if any are to be strictly followed.

However, above procedure is subject to modification of policies and procedures circulated by the Government of West Bengal time to time.

##  C. Special Provision for purchase of Tribal Land:

As per the legal provision, Permission is to be obtained from the District Authority specified for this purpose. The tribal landowner has to apply to concerned District Authority attaching details and copies of documents with respect to the land he desires to sell. If he wants to nominate any non-tribal person for employment than same is to be specifically mentioned in the application for approval.

After obtaining above approval, procedure is to be followed as described above for purchase of Tenancy land.





#  PF-3

##  IMPORTANT STEPS IN TRANSFER OF GOVERNMENT LAND AND 'PATTA-LAND' THROUGH ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual submission of application for transfer of land. Process-Flow described below is being followed in the State of West Bengal only. State of Jharkhand has separate lease policy for transfer of Government land to Central Government Companies at the time of preparation of the SOP. Procedure as circulated and practiced by the concerned State Government should be followed)

##  A. For transfer of encumbrance-free Government Land

A. Proposal is submitted by the General Manager of the concerned Area at District Land Acquisition office requesting transfer of government land attaching:

I. Details of the mine/ project for which the land is required;

II. Copy of relevant pages approved PR/ Scheme of the mine which shows requirement of the land;

III. Details of the required Government land;

IV. Plan showing location of the propose land with respect to the mine/ project.

B. Area State Revenue department and ADSR office separately calculate value of the land. Higher of the two is considered and Transfer value is determined by the District Authority.

C. Then, the proposal is sent by the Collector with recommendation for lease/ perpetual transfer of the land, as the case may be, to State Headquarter for approval.

D. After approval of the proposal by the State Government, District Land Acquisition Authority raise demand.

E. After obtaining demand from the District Authority, Area General Manager has to pay the demanded amount from budget available in the Area under the specific head for the particular project/ mine. In case of insufficiency of fund in the Area the same is to be obtained from corporate level by initiating specific proposal for the purpose attaching all relevant documents like copy of Scheme/ Project Report, Plan of the project showing location of the land, demand of the State Government, documents related to land, copy of approval of the land, copy of approved schedule of land etc.

F. If the demanded amount surpasses the provision of Capital provided earlier then fresh approval is to be obtained for payment of the demanded amount from the same Authority who accorded earlier approval.

G. After payment of the demanded of amount, District Land acquisition Authority hands over possession of land to Area Authority. Representatives of District land acquisition Authority and Area/ Project Authority signs on the said possession document.

H. A deed of conveyance in specified format supplied by the District authority is signed by the representatives of both State Government and ECL and is registered for transfer of land.

I. Mutation of land is done in favour of ECL.

##  B. For Patta Land that has not been acquired under Coal Bearing Areas (Acquisition and Development) Act 1957, The Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 or any other State land acquisition Act:

ECL Board has approved following modus operandi in its $296^{th}$ meeting for acquisition of Government land on which Patta has been issued by the State Government:



1. The Patta holder will submit application claiming employment and/or other R&R benefits furnishing details of land being offered for the claim and attaching copies of Patta certificate, ROR and other ownership documents, if any, to concerned Mine Authority requesting acquisition of the land under his possession.

2. Area Authority and the Patta holder will sign on an Agreement [as per the standard draft of the agreement approved in $296^{th}$ Meeting of ECL Board (Form XX)] that, Patta holder will get employment and/or other R&R benefits admissible to him with the conditions that –

i. Patta holder will voluntarily apply to the concerned SDO for cancellation/annulment of the Patta;

ii. Patta holder will relinquish his Patta right and will handover encumbrance-free possession of the land to the State Government;

iii. If Patta holder has less than 2.00 acres of land then, he will be able to club together his land with land of other landowners within the chunk of land wherein his land lies and which has been approved for purchase along with employment under package deal concept to the extent of two acres and nominate one of the land losers among the groups or their dependent for employment;

iv. ECL Will apply to the concerned District Authority for transfer of the land as soon as possible after cancellation of Patta;

v. His application for employment will be processed for approval after cancellation of his Patta. But his appointment will be released only after handing over possession of the concerned Govt. on which Patta was issued earlier to him land by concerned State Govt. authority to ECL.

3. Patta holder will also submit the checklist no. CL-4 and documents as per the said checklist including an Affidavit [as per the standard draft of the affidavit approved in $296^{th}$ Meeting of ECL Board(Form XIX)] sworn before first class executive Magistrate stating name of the nominee of employment, total schedule of land for which employment has been claimed, genuiness of copies of all the documents submitted along with his claim of employment and his acceptance of the conditions mentioned in the aforesaid agreement.

4. Patta holder will submit application attaching copies of Patta Documents and Record of Rights to the Sub-Divisional Officer for cancelation of his Patta.

5. After observing the procedure laid down in the statute and guideline issued by the State Government SDO will annul/ cancel the Patta.

6. After obtaining copy of the cancelation/annulment of the Patta, Area General Manager will submit application for transfer of the government land on which Patta has been cancelled/annulled. All the steps mentioned above for transfer of encumbrance-free Government land will be executed for transfer of the land.

7. Simultaneously, the Patta Holder may apply (in Form-I) attaching all relevant documents specified in ‘Activity Step 2’ and copy of the cancelation certificate issued by the SDO.

8. The employment proposal will be examined subject to R&R Policy and prevalent norms of the company. It will be processed through Area Land cell committee as per normal procedure and should be specifically recommended by the General Manager of the Area. Procedures depicted under Steps 4,5,6 and 7 will followed for approval of the employment.

9. Approved employment proposal will be forwarded to General Manager (P&IR), ECL for further examination like verification of testimonials, examination of medical fitness, police verification etc. as done for other employment proposals against land; but, appointment letter will be released only after obtaining certificate from the General Manager of concerned Area that ECL has received possession from the State Government authority with respect to the Government land, on which Patta was issued earlier to the concerned applicant.

PROCESS FLOW FOR ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER RIGHT TO FAIR COMPENSATION AND TRANSPARENCY IN LAND ACQUISITION, REHABILITATION AND RESETTEMENT ACT, 2013

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP etc. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual submission of application for acquisition of land under the aforementioned Act)

1. Acquisition of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 will be done by Gazette Notifications according to the statutory provisions and procedures as laid down in the Act and quantum of land to be acquired should be limited to the provision of the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report of the Mine/ Project.

2. If particulars of the land to be acquired under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 is not clearly mentioned in the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report then, a specific approval of CMD ECL is to be obtained by initiating a proposal.

3. Schedule of the land and graphical representation of the same on Revenue/ Mouza map are to be prepared for acquisition under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

4. Above schedule is to be verified with computerized and physical records of the concerned collieries to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier.

5. Above schedule is to be reconciled with land records of neighboring mines/ Areas in 'FORM-VII' to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier.

6. If rehabilitation of Project Affected Families is proposed then all the information and documents as mentioned in CHECK LIST NO. 3 as mentioned are to be furnished and requisite documents to substantiate the information are to be attached.

7. Proposal is to be initiated by the mine management (signed by Surveyor, land dealing official, safety officer, Project officer/ Agent) and will be forwarded to the General Manager of the concerned Area attaching documents as per CHECK LIST NO. 1.3 for obtaining competent approval with respect to –

a. Acquisition of scheduled land under the said Act.

b. Processing and submitting application to the District land Acquisition Authority for acquisition of the land.

c. Approximate requirement of fund that will be needed to meet 'Cost of Acquisition' which will be demanded by the Land acquisition Authority afterwards and any other cost associated to it.

d. Capturing satellite image of the total acquired land including the settlements/villages which lie within the proposed area of acquisition immediately after Notification with respect to declaration of acquisition by the State Government u/s 19.

8. The land acquisition proposal along with the rehabilitation programme is to be examined and recommended by 'Area Land Cell' (in FORM- XXII) and by the Area General Manager. Then it should be forwarded to General Manager (LRE), Headquarter.

9. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the General Manager (Planning), General Manager (Safety) and General Manager (Finance) In-charge. Then the proposal is to be forwarded for consent of Director (Technical)



Project & Planning, Director (Technical) Operation, Director (Finance) and approval of CMD, ECL

10. After obtaining approval of competent authority, Area General Manager will submit formal application along with required plans and documents to District Land Acquisition Authority for acquisition of specified quantum of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

11. Area General Manager will authorize suitable executive (s) who will remain actively involved in the land acquisition activities, monitor progress of the same and intimate it to Area General Manager, keep liaison with the Land acquisition and State/ National monitoring authorities, attend the related meetings, participate in the proceedings held before the Collector/Court for determining the amount of compensation and to protect the interests of the Company by adducing evidence and put across views/ objections of ECL wherever necessary in those meetings. He will communicate Names, designations, places of posting and contact details of those executives to the Collector along with the application.

12. Procedure for acquisition of land, rehabilitation and resettlement of PAFs etc. will be followed as laid down in the Act. However, Area General Manager will take prompt action to deposit the amount demanded by Land acquisition Authority on account of ‘Cost of Acquisition’ within stipulated time.

13. After publication of Notification under section 19 Area General Manager will send written request to General Manager (P&P) and General Manager (Systems) attaching required plan and other information like latitude/ longitude of the acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the area of acquisition for capturing Satellite image of the same area.

14. Executives/ team, authorized to monitor the acquisition process, will examine and ensure that resettlement benefit is limited to summary of R&R published u/s 19 as well as the satellite image captured immediately after it.

15. If employment is generated and the same is admissible to any 'affected family' then employment proposal may be processed after completion of the total acquisition process and possession of land is handed over by the State Government Authority to ECL.

16. Landowner/ 'Affected Family' will submit application in Form-I claiming employment along with all the documents mentioned in Activity Step 2. Processing of employment proposal will be as per activity steps 4,5,6 and 7.



#  IMPORTANT STEPS IN ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

Proposal is moved by the General Manager of the concerned Area for acquisition of land under the said Act attaching Statements and certificates in Form 1A, 1B, 1C and 1D regarding availability of coal, verification of land details as per State Government records, that no mineral lease has been obtained earlier on the said area, Draft Notification etc. After obtaining approval of the proposal by competent authority online Application is uploaded in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 4 of the Act

Notification u/s 4(1): Publication of preliminary notification for prospecting purpose

Notification u/s 7(1): Notice showing Intention of the Central Government to acquire part or whole area of the land notified u/s 4 for coal mining. Plot-wise details are provided in the notification. Maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year.

Notification u/s 8(1): Any 'person interested' may lodge a complaint over any portion of land notified u/s 7(1) within thirty days of the said Notification. Notified 'Competent Authority' (Coal Controller at present). Will dispose off the complaints after hearing and his specific recommendation for decision of the Central Government

Notification u/s 9(1): Declaration by the Central Government regarding acquisition of the notified land area

Section 10: By virtue of this section the land notified u/s 9(1) is vested absolutely in the Central Government encumbrance free

Notification u/s 11 (1): Notice for vesting the land, which was vested in the Central Government by virtue of Section 10, is vested in the Government Company.

Notice u/s 12: Power of the competent authority (notified u/s 3) to take possession of the land. He will give notice to the landowner/ ‘person interested’ to hand over possession of the land within such period as he deem fit. In case of any hindrance law and order machinery may be used.



Section 13(5): compensation to be paid to the 'person interested' based on the market value and other damages in the law.

As per Guideline of Central Government: Compensation amount u/s 13 should consist of (a) Market value of land as on the date of notification u/s 4, (b) Solatium @ 30% on the market value of land (c) Interest amount @ 12% for the period between notification u/s 4 and 9 (Max. three years) + @ 9% for one year after notification u/s 9 and @ 15% thenafter till payment is made

Section 14(1): Payment of compensation may be made as per agreed rate. If no agreement could be reached, the matter may be resolved by award given by a Tribunal specially formed for the purpose (For ECL it is at Godda Jharkhand)

Section 17: Central Government has to pay compensation to the ‘person interested’. By Gazette Notification, S.O. 2378 dated $13^{th}$ October 2017 Central Government has delegated this power to CIL and its subsidiaries

Gazette Notification 2368(E) Dated 28th August 2015 [RFCTLARR (Removal of Difficulty Order)]:Central Government has Notified that Compensation and R&R benefits for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 has to be provided as per First, Second and Third Schedule of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.This is effected from 1st September 2015.

As per clarification provided by MoC:

1. Collector is the competent Authority for determination of compensation u/s 26 of RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

2. If determination of compensation has been done before the cut-off date (1-9-15) then the same cannot be reopened.

3. Fresh agreement with ‘affected family’ u/s 14(1) may be executed if it is more beneficial to him.
