

#  STEP 6: VERIFICATION AND PROCESSING OF EMPLOYMENT PROPOSAL AT AREA LEVEL

##  Activity 6.0: Examination/verification and recommendation of the employment proposal by the Area Authority



| Sl. No. | SUB - ACTIVITIES |
| --- | --- |
| A | R.O.R of the offered plot (s) are to be thoroughly examined and verified by the Area Land Dealing Officials. Information furnished in 'Attached Composite Summary Sheet' (Form-XI), Form-IX and Form-X is to be verified with committee vetted 'Computerized Land inventory record' maintained in prescribed tabular format. A certificate is to given by the Area Land Dealing Officer in Form-XXI stating that the information furnished in the said forms are correct and have been verified with updated Computerized Land record maintained in prescribed tabular format; photocopies of documents have been verified with the originals; and the land offered for employment in the proposal is within the approved land schedule and within total approved area of the individual plot. |
| B | Attach copy of the FORM - XIII (previous 18 point proforma) along with (A) Notice for conducting verification of the photocopies of documents with the originals submitted by the candidate as per said form. (B) Attach Report of the said verification/scrutiny. |
| C | If the nominated candidate is a female, the Area Land Cell Committee should properly verify and justify her claim and Area General Manager should himself counsel the applicant and report of the same is to be enclosed (ref of G.M (LRE) letter no. ECL/CM.D/LRE/ATR/642, dated-23.06.2017) along with specific recommendation with respect to non availability of 'eligible male candidate' in the family of concerned landowner/ 'person interested' for claiming employment and suitability of the female candidate for employment in ECL. |
| D | Attach copy of Police verification report with respect to relationship between landowner and nominee or 'on-the-spot relationship verification certificate' in Form-XV and Form XVA. |
| D | All enclosed documents attached to the proposal should be serially numbered and countersigned and all the photocopies of documents are to be attested by the Area Land Dealing Officer mentioning his/ her full name, designation, date and seal. |
| E | A check list in prescribed tabular form (CL-5) and attach all documents mentioned therein |
| F | Proposal is to be recommended if found in order, by the Area General Manager in line with the order circulated vide office order no-ECL/D(P)/77/67/126, on 07.06.2008 and the same is to be forwarded to the Head of LRE Department, Headquarter for processing and approval. |
