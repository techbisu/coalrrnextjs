

#  STEP 7: PROCESSING OF EMPLOYMENT PROPOSAL AT HEAD QUARTER

##  Activity 7.0: Examination, recommendation and approval of the employment proposal at corporate Head quarter



| SI. No. | SUB - AVTIVITIES |
| --- | --- |
| A | The employment proposal should be examined by the concerned dealing officer, LRE HQ. He will initiate a note certifying that the proposal has been prepared, certified and recommended by the unit/project/area authority (including Area Land Cell Committee, Area General Manager ) as per the approved SOP, check-list has been duly filled and all the required documents as per SOP have been properly attached, countersigned and verified by the assigned Authorities. He will also certify that, except for the exempt cases like land acquired under CBA Act, 1957, cases dealing with approved conditional employment against subjudice land, land acquired at places where CNT Act or SPT Act are applicable etc, the plots offered for instant employment has been mutated correctly in the name of ECL and the proposal is in line with R&R Policy of CIL (specify the reference of approval obtained from the Competent Authority for relaxation of any provision of R&R policy of CIL or existing norm of the company, if any in this regard. Attach a copy of the said approval also). He will also attach Synopsis of information regarding the employment in a prescribed Format (FORM-XIV). |
| B | The proposal along with the note of dealing officer, HQ is to be forwarded to a 3-Member Committee consisting of authorised representatives of LRE, HOD (Legal) and HOD (Planning) for examination and recommendation. The committee will also recommend the information furnished in FORM-XIV for publication in official website of ECL. |
| C | After recommendation of the 3-Member Committee, General Manager (LRE) will forward the proposal to concerned Department for uploading the information furnished in FORM-XIV and keeping the same in the official website of ECL for at least twenty one days for intimation to general people and submission of further information/ complaint in this regard within three weeks of publication in the official website. People will be asked to submit their written complaint/ additional information along with supporting documents at the office of the General Manager (LRE). |







| SI. No. | SUB - AVTIVITIES |
| --- | --- |
| D | If any complaint/ additional information is received then, General Manager (LRE) will return the proposal to concerned Area General Manager for enquiry and resolving the issue. After disposing of the complaint(s) Area General Manager will again forward the proposal to the General Manager (LRE) for further processing. |
| E | General Manager (LRE) will forward the proposal along with his own recommendation to the Competent Authority for approval. |
| F | After approval of the proposal by the Competent Authority, the proposal file along with the attached documents will be forwarded to HOD (Personnel) for carrying out other formalities like medical fitness examination, verification of testimonials and antecedents etc After complying with said general norms and specific conditions related to the instant proposal, if any, he will issue appointment letter to the nominee offering employment. |
