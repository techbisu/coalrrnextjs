
#  PF-1

##  PROCESS FLOW FOR ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual acquisition of land)

1. Schedule of the land and graphical representation of the same on Revenue/ Mouza map are to be prepared for acquisition under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957. Total quantum of land must be within approved limit mentioned in Project Report/ Scheme (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

2. Above schedule is to be verified with computerized and physical records of the concerned collieries to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

3. Above schedule is to be reconciled with land records of neighboring mines/ Areas in 'FORM-VII' to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier (Follow Activity Step 1.1.A).

4. Proposal is initiated by the mine management (signed by Surveyor, land dealing official, safety officer, Project officer/ Agent) and will be forwarded to the General Manager of the concerned Area attaching

i. Copy of approval of the Project Report/ Scheme accorded by competent authority;

ii. Copy of relevant pages of the Project Report/ Scheme (if available or reason for non-availability),

iii. Copy of Geological report,

iv. schedule of Mouza-wise land areas to be acquired,

v. Five copies of Mouza maps showing specified areas in different mouzas that has been proposed for acquisition,

vi. statements and certificates in Form 1A, 1B, 1C and 1D and

vii. Any other document which will be needed for notification under Section 4 of the Act,

viii. Soft copies of all above documents.

for obtaining competent approval with respect to –

a. Acquisition of scheduled land under the said Act.

b. Processing and submitting application to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India for Notification under section 4, 7, 9 and 11 of the Act.

c. For publishing intention of acquisition of scheduled land in company website and in two dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular once after Notification under section 7 and once after Notification under section 11.

d. For carrying out socio-economic survey by a committee/ expert agency within two months of Notification under section 7 for identification of PAFs/ displaced families and recording baseline information and data regarding the same.

e. Capturing satellite image of the total acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the proposed area of acquisition immediately after Notification u/s 9.

5. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by the Area Land Cell and Area General Manager. Then it should be forwarded to General Manager (LRE), Headquarter.

6. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the General Manager (Project & Planning), General Manager (Safety) and General Manager (Finance) In-charge. Then the proposal is to be forwarded for consent of Director (Technical) Project & Planning, Director (Technical) Operation, Director (Finance) and approval of CMD, ECL

7. After obtaining competent approval of the proposal by competent authority, online Application will be uploaded in the specified Central Government Portal by General Manager (LRE) for publication of



Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 4 of the Act. Hard copy of the same may also be forwarded to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India, if needed, as per existing guideline/ practice for the said purpose.

8. After publication of Notification under section 4 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Submit all documents like, detailed schedule of plots for acquisition, Copy of relevant pages of the Project Report/ Scheme, Copy of Geological report, Five copies of Mouza maps showing specified areas in different mouzas that has been proposed for acquisition statements and certificates in prescribed Form 2A, 2B, 2C, 2D etc. and Any other document which will be needed for notification under Section 7 of the Act. (Care should be taken to complete this step as early as possible because maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year).

II. Will entrust suitable officials of his Area for collection of data and information regarding ownership of the lands and getting the same authenticated by the concerned authorities of District revenue department.

9. General Manager (LRE) will arrange to submit online Application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in ‘Gazette of India’ u/s 7 of the Act.

10. After publication of Notification under section 7 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Submit all documents required for Notification under section 9 to General Manager (LRE).

II. Publish the content of Notification u/s 7 along with plot-schedule of land in website of the company and in two newspapers, one in well circulated English daily and one in well circulated local vernacular.

III. Form a committee involving Collector of the District or appoint an expert agency for carrying out socio-economic survey for identification of PAFs/ displaced families and recording baseline information and data regarding the same. The said survey should be completed within two months of Notification under section 7.

IV. Send one original copy and one photocopy of the above newspaper publications to General Manager (LRE).

11. General Manager (LRE) will forward application along with photocopy of Notification u/s 7 and original copy of the newspaper publications to the Competent Authority (Coal Controller or any other Authority as notified by the Central Government) for issuance of report u/s 8.

12. General Manager (LRE) will submit online application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 9 of the Act. He will also forward a copy of the report of 'Competent Authority' u/s 8 of the Act to Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India (If the said Authority does not send the report directly to Ministry of Coal).

13. After publication of Notification under section 9 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. submit all documents required for Notification under section 11 to General Manager (LRE).

II. Send written request to General Manager (P&P) and General Manager (Systems) attaching required plan and other information like latitude/ longitude of the acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the area of acquisition for capturing Satellite image of the same area.

14. General Manager (P&P) with the help of General Manager (Systems) will arrange to capture Satellite image, as requested, immediately and send copies of the said image to Area General Manager and to General Manager (LRE).

15. If Rehabilitation of PAFs are involved then Area General Manager will take immediate actions as per Activity Step- 8 of the SOP for conducting census and socio-economic study for collection of baseline data and issuance of identity cards to the ‘Displaced Families.’



16. General Manager (LRE) will arrange to submit online Application in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 11 of the Act.

17. After publication of Notification under section 11 General Manager (LRE) will forward a copy of the same to the Area General Manager. After that, Area General Manager will –

I. Publish the content of Notification u/s 11 along with plot-schedule of land as notified u/s 9 in website of the company and in two newspapers, one in well circulated English daily and one in well circulated local vernacular.

II. Paste copies of Notification u/s 11 along with plot-schedule of land as notified u/s 9 at Panchayet Bhawnas and at other conspicuous places in the mouzas where acquisition has taken place.

III. Proclaim the same in and nearby areas/villages by beat of drum.

IV. If Rehabilitation of PAFs are involved then he will take immediate actions as per approved 'Rehabilitation Planning Guideline' or in absence of the same for preparation and consent with respect to 'Scheme for Rehabilitation' as described in Activity Step- 8 of the SOP.

V. For Rehabilitation of PAFs Area General Manager will also take steps for constitution of Resettlement & Rehabilitation Committee as described in Activity Step- 8.B.6 of the SOP.

18. Landowner will submit application in Form-I claiming compensation and R&R benefits along with all the documents mentioned in Activity Step 2.

19. Simultaneously, A formal proposal is to be initiated by the Mine/ project for taking physical possession of land which has been acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 with all the documents and plans mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.B.

20. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by Area Land Cell as well as Area General Manager. (Step 1.1.C)

21. Abstract of land (in Form-III) intended for purchasing is to be kept published, for at least three weeks, on official website of ECL inviting further information/ complaint from general mass with respect to the scheduled plots. Hard copy the publication is to be notified in the official Notice Board of the Unit/ Area and at conspicuous places of concerned villages and to be published in two widely circulated dailies, one in English and one in local vernacular (as per Activity Step 1.1.D).

22. Area General Manager or a suitable Authority duly authorized by him for the purpose has to dispose off the complaints/ disputes within two weeks after expiry of three weeks of publication in website or receipt of complaint whichever is earlier (as per Step 1.1.E). He will forward the proposal to GM (LRE) with specific recommendation and modified schedule after resolution of disputes. List of the disputed land may also be attached, if possible, for which compensation will have to be deposited at tribunal (as per Activity Step 1.1.E).

23. GM (LRE) will forward the proposal to GM (Project & Planning) and GM (Fin) I/C. They will examine the proposal in light of approved project parameters, year-wise production plan and financial criteria of the project respectively and will recommend accordingly (as per Activity Step 1.1.F).

24. After this, GM (LRE) will forward the proposal for consent of concerned Director (Tech), Director (Personnel) and Director (Fin) with his specific recommendation. Then, the same will be forwarded to CMD, ECL for approval if the number of employment and other parameters are within the provisions of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report. If the proposed parameters exceed the provisions of approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report then the proposal is to be placed in meeting of Board of Directors of ECL for approval (as per Activity Step 1.1.G).

25. A Compensation Payroll (Details in Standard Form 1A and Abstract in standard Form 1B) for the concerned landowners is to be prepared by the unit under section 13(5) or section 14(1) of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 as the case may be. For Compensation Payroll prepared under Section 13(5) of the Act Government notified market value prevalent on the date of determination of compensation is to be considered. Market value is to be determined as per the provision of Section 26 of the RFCTLARR Act,



2013. The Compensation Payroll with a formal proposal will be forwarded to the Area General Manager attaching information/ documents mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.H.

26. The proposal is to be examined and recommended by Area Land Cell as well as Area General Manager and will be forwarded to GM (LRE). Conditions mentioned in Activity Step 1.1.I are to be followed. Statutory provisions and related clarifications issued by the Central/ State Government are to be obeyed strictly.

27. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the GM (Project & Planning) and to General Manager (Finance) and forward the same with specific recommendation to the CMD, ECL for approval after obtaining consent for the same from concerned Director (Tech) and Director (Fin) (as per Activity Step 1.1.J).

28. General Manager (LRE) will forward the approved compensation payroll to the Area General Manager for publication of the award u/s 13(5) or 14(1) in the public domain of official website of ECL and pasting the same at conspicuous places of the concerned villages asking the landowners to handover physical possession of land receiving awarded compensation and other allowances and submission of application for employment if he is entitled to. Every such published Award must have a unique Number and Date (as per Activity Step 1.1.K).

29. It shall be mentioned in the published Award that, all the notified landowners have to claim the compensation amount submitting bank account details for RTGS along with self-signed Form-I and associated documents and that, it may be deposited in the tribunal if they fail to do so within two months of publication of the Award in official website of ECL.

30. Landowners/ affected families may submit application along with all documents as per Activity Step 2.

31. Area Authority will start disbursement of awarded compensation amount and provide approved R&R benefits to the concerned landowner/ persons interested/ affected families based on their submitted claims and documents, entitlements as per the provisions of Statute and approvals obtained in this regard. Procedure described in Activity Step 1.1.M and 3.1 is to be followed.

32. Physical possession of Land should be taken as per the finally approved schedule of the land providing compensation and benefits as mentioned in the Award.

33. Area General Manager should also accord administrative approval for deposition of compensation amount to the tribunal for disputed/ sub-judice lands and those lands for which no claims are submitted by the landowners. Physical possession of such land should be taken after deposition of awarded compensation amount at the concerned tribunal. Area land dealing officer or any other executive of the Area nominated by Area General Manager for the purpose will be responsible for depositing the compensation amount within three months of obtaining above approval from the Area General Manager.

34. All the information with respect to compensation paid and R&R Benefits provided must be entered in FORM D and kept recorded in a bound paged book ('COMPENSATION AND R&R REGISTER'). The same is also to be preserved in computer in the same Format. Entries of the said register are to be signed by colliery land dealing official, manager and finance executive. Manager will communicate the said entries to the Area Land dealing officer. Area Land dealing officer will maintain copy of the same register and will sign on it (as per Activity Step 1.1.N).

35. Employment and other R&R Benefits will be provided following the procedure mentioned in Activity Steps 4,5,6, and 7.

#  CLARIFICATIONS

1. For land acquired before 1st September 2015, compensation payment will be guided by the clarifications circulated by Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India dated 4th August 2017,



2. Clarifications circulated by Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India dated 30th March 2018 and 28th June 2019 are to be followed also. [Important aspects have been provided in the below section named as ‘IMPORTANT GAZETTE NOTIFICATIONS AND CLARIFICATIONS OF CENTRAL GOVERNMENT’]

3. Where compensation has been determined and cases cannot be reopened as clarifications circulated by Ministry of Coal, Govt. of India dated $4^{th}$ August 2017 or compensation has to be paid as per agreement rate under section 14(1) of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, calculation of compensation u/s 13(5) is to be done abiding by earlier guideline of Central Government in this respect [Please see below section named as 'IMPORTANT SECTIONS OF CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957'] or as per the agreed rate and terms and conditions u/s 14(1) as the case may be.

4. Where compensation is paid u/s 14(1) after the cut-off date i.e. 1st September 2015, the affected family has to swear an affidavit in front of First-Class Magistrate regarding his refusal of awarded compensation and his acceptance of the agreed compensation. He has to enter into an Agreement with ECL for this purpose (Agreement Form XXIV).

5. Where compensation is to be determined as per First schedule of RFCTLARR Act, 2013, compensation amount may be determined as per guidance provided in Appendix-D.

6. General Manager of the concerned Area, being Competent Authority as per notification u/s 3 of RFCTLARR Act, 2013, may use the services of experienced persons in the field of agriculture, forestry, horticulture, sericulture, or any other field, as may be considered necessary by him for determination of market value of trees and other immovable property/ assets and damage on account of standing crops attached to the acquired land.

7. Identification of ‘Affected Family’/Recipients of benefit with respect to ‘One-Time Payment of Rs. Five lakh’/ Annuity as per the provision of Second Schedule of RFCTLARR Act, 2013, where jobs are not created, is to be done as per legal opinion of Additional Solicitor General, Ranchi High Court and Additional advocate General, Ranchi (Attached as LP1 and LP2).

8. Any claim lodged by any livelihood-loser as an "Affected Family' under the meaning of section 3 (ii), (iii), (iv) and (vi) of RFCTLARR Act, 2013 can be considered for settlement, strictly as per provisions of the said Act, only if concerned Collector of the District/ District Forest Officer authenticates that total annual income of the claimant was dependent on the acquired land (specifying details of the land) at least for previous three years before acquisition had taken place.

#  IMPORTANT SECTS OF CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

Notification u/s 4(1): Publication of preliminary notification for prospecting purpose

Notification u/s 7(1): Notice showing Intention of the Central Government to acquire part or whole area of the land notified u/s 4 for coal mining. Plot-wise details are provided in the notification. Maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year.

Notification u/s 8(1): Any 'person interested' may lodge a complaint over any portion of land notified u/s 7(1) within thirty days of the said Notification. Notified 'Competent Authority' (Coal Controller at present). Will dispose off the complaints after hearing and his specific recommendation for decision of the Central Government

Notice u/s 12: Power of the competent authority (notified u/s 3) to take possession of the land. He will give notice to the landowner/ ‘person interested’ to hand over possession of the land within such period as he deems fit. In case of any hindrance law and order machinery may be used.



Section 13(5): compensation to be paid to the 'person interested' based on the market value and other damages in the law.

As per Guideline of Central Government: Compensation amount u/s 13 should consist of (a) Market value of land as on the date of notification u/s 4, (b) Solatium @ 30% on the market value of land (c) Interest amount   12% for the period between notification u/s 4 and 9 (Max. three years) +   9% for one year after notification u/s 9 and @ 15% then after till payment is made

Section 14(1): Payment of compensation may be made as per agreed rate. If no agreement could be reached, the matter may be resolved by award given by a Tribunal specially formed for the purpose (For ECL it is at Godda Jharkhand)

Section 17: Central Government has to pay compensation to the 'person interested'. By Gazette Notification, S.O. 2378 dated $13^{th}$ October 2017 Central Government has delegated this power to CIL and its subsidiaries

#  IMPORTANT GAZETTE NOTIFICATIONS AND CLARIFICATIONS OF CENTRAL GOVERNMENT:

Gazette Notification 2368(E) Dated 28th August 2015 [RFCTLARR (Removal of Difficulty Order)]:Central Government has Notified that Compensation and R&R benefits for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 has to be provided as per First, Second and Third Schedule of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.This is effected from 1st September 2015.

As per clarification provided by MoC (dated $4^{th}$ August 2017, $30^{th}$ March 2018 and $28^{th}$ June 2019):

1. General Manager of the concerned Area, being Competent Authority as per notification u/s 3 of RFCTLARR Act, 2013, is the competent Authority for determination of compensation for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957.

2. If determination of compensation has been done before the cut-off date ($1^{st}$ September 2015) then the same cannot be reopened irrespective of whether possession has been taken or not.

3. Where prior to 01.09.2015, declaration of acquisition has been published under CBA Act, but determination of compensation has not been made under Sec. 13(5) of the CBA Act, compensation will be determined following the provisions of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013 irrespective of whether possession has been taken or not

4. Fresh agreement with ‘affected family’ u/s 14(1) may be executed if it is more beneficial to him.
