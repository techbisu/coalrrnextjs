The said proposal should consist of following documents/ information: |
| --- | --- |
| SI. No. | Sub Activity |
| 4.A | Application by nominee addressing the Head of the Colliery/Project Authority attaching details in Form V for providing employment to himself/ herself against his/her land and/ or land offered by other landowners as per R&R policy of the CIL narrating details of the land offered which has been duly acquired/ purchased by ECL, and has been mutated in favour of ECL and physical possession of the same has been handed over to the company. |
| 4.B | Bio-data of the Nominee mentioning his/her name, Father's/Husband's name, Present & Permanent Address, Contact No, Email ID (if available), Date of Birth, Educational / Professional Qualification, Gender, Nationality, Religion, Whether belonging to SC/ST/OBC community, Voter Card & Aadhaar No, whether the applicant is related to any existing employee of ECL or any of the subsidiary of CIL, if any (mention the name, EIN/ Unique Man Number, designation, place of posting and his/her relationship with the nominee of such related employee, if applicable) [self-attested copy of all support documents to be enclosed] |
| 4.C | Duly signed and properly filled-up FORM-A, Self-declaration Certificate (FORM-VI), and Standard Attestation Form enclosing all supporting documents as mentioned in the said prescribed forms. |
| 4.D | Copy of the publication in ECL Website regarding purchase/possession of land. |
| 4.E | Declaration in the form of an Affidavit (solemnised before the First Class Magistrate) submitted by the applicant/ nominee of the employment mentioning details of offered land and attaching a proper plan demarcating the land offered for his/her employment and stating that he/she has handed over encumbrance free physical possession of the land involved in his/her employment to ECL on the field and if any dispute arises in future, which challenges ownership of ECL over the said land or impedes peaceful possession of ECL over the land then, ECL will be free to take any disciplinary action against him including termination of his service. |

| 4.F | Declaration in the form of an Affidavit (solemnised before the First Class Magistrate) - submitted by the landowner/ Nominator mentioning details of the person nominated by him for employment along with details of the land offered by him for the same purpose and stating that the offered plot(s) is/are presently (1) free from any dispute with respect to ownership of land with the co-sharer (s), (2) free from Bargadar or any other encumbrance (3) No case of any nature, related to offered land, is pending at any court of law or tribunal (4) He or his predecessors never sold the land, wholly or partially, earlier to ECL or any other entity (organisation/ person) and he or his family has not received any employment benefit against the concerned land. If part of the plot has been transferred earlier to any other entity then documentary proof is to be attached that the particular area of plot which is being offered for the current employment, was not transferred earlier (5) He or his legal heirs will not claim employment from ECL against the offered land in future. |
| 4.G | An Indemnity Bond solemnized before a First Class Executive Magistrate to be given by the candidate/ nominee with sureties, who will be a permanent employee of ECL having at least five years remaining service, is to be submitted mentioning categorically that all documents furnished by him/her are genuine and authentic. If it is found that employment has been received by adopting any unfair practice, suppressing any document, submission of any forged document, or misleading the Company by any means - then in that case service of the nominee will be terminated and any damage suffered by ECL for such act of the applicant will be duly indemnified by the nominee of the employment. |
| 4.H | All pages of photocopies of documents submitted by the nominee/candidate along with his application for employment must be countersigned by him with date and a Remark, "Submitted by me and is a photocopy of original". These documents are to be countersigned by Area Land dealing officer mentioning his/ her full name, designation, date and seal. |
