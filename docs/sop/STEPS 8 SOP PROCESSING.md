

#  STEP 8: APPROVAL FOR REHABILITATION PACKAGE FOR PAFS WHO WILL BE SHIFTED FROM PROJECT AREA

##  Activity 8.0: Approval for rehabilitation package for PAFs to be rehabilitated for approved project activities

##  S. No. |SUB - AVTIVITIES

A
If the PAFs are to be shifted from a land which is acquired under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 then, Rehabilitation is done by the State Land Acquisition Authority. Area General Manager has to deposit the amount on account of payment of compensation and other R&R benefits as demanded by the Land Acquisition Authority under the provision of the Act. Procedure depicted in Activity 1.4 are to be followed before starting of the acquisition of land and rehabilitation of the PAFs. If demanded amount is beyond the limit of Budget provided to the Area then, Area Authority will take immediate step to collect additional budget required for the purpose from Head Quarters so that the demanded amount can be deposited to the District Authority within time stipulated in the Act.

If the PAFs are to be shifted from a land which is acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 or through outright purchase by registration of sale deed then,

1. Census is to be carried out within two months of publication of notification U/S 7(1) of CBA (A.D) Act 1957 or approval of the Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report for acquisition of land for the Project/ Mine in case of outright purchase of land, a census would be undertaken in the manner to be decided by the Collector / project authority for identification of displaced families and for preparing their socio-economic profile and list of eligible persons for the purpose of receiving Rehabilitation & Resettlement Benefits.

2. A baseline socioeconomic survey will be carried out to identify the PAPs who are enlisted to receive benefits in line with Coal India's Resettlement and Rehabilitation Policy. This survey will be conducted within two months of notification under the relevant land acquisition Acts or approval of the Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report for acquisition of land for the Project/ Mine in case of outright purchase of land by the subsidiaries with the help of reputed independent institutional agencies, who are well versed with the social matrix of the area.

B The basic objective of the socio-economic study will be to generate baseline data on the social and economic status of the population who are likely to lose their means of livelihood or homestead due to the acquisition of the land for the project. The data base will be used to formulate a viable and practical Rehabilitation Action Plan (RAP) for the affected persons in line with their entitlements.

3. A photo identity card to each Entitled Project Affected Person shall be issued under the signature of the Collector / project authority concerned indicating the following particulars:

(a) Name of the village/GP/PS:

(b) Name, Father's name and address of the head of the family;

(c) Category of entitlement

(d) Whether S.C./S.T./O.B.C./General:

(e) Age, Sex, educational qualification of the members of the family
