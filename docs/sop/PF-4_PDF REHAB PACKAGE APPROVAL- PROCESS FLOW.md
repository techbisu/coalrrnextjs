
#  PF-4

##  STEPS FOR APPROVAL OF REHABILITATION PACKAGE OF PAFS

###  1. UNDER RFCTLARR ACT 2013:

Rehabilitation is done by the State Land Acquisition Authority. Area General Manager has to deposit the amount on account of payment of compensation and other R&R benefits as demanded by the Land Acquisition Authority under the provision of the Act. Procedure depicted in Activity 1.4 are to be followed before starting of the acquisition of land and rehabilitation of the PAFs. If demanded amount is beyond the limit of Budget provided to the Area then, Area Authority will take immediate step to collect additional budget required for the purpose from Head Quarter so that the demanded amount can be deposited to the District Authority within time stipulated in the Act.

###  2. UNDER CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957 OR THROUGH OUTRIGHT PURCHASE BY REGISTRATION OF SALE DEED:

If the PAFs are to be shifted from a land which is acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 or through outright purchase by registration of sale deed then (Following provisions no. 1.2 and 3 are as per R&R Policy of Coal India Limited, 2012. Provisions will be governed by the R&R Policy of Coal India Limited prevalent on the date of acquisition/ possession of land,

1. Within two months of publication of notification U/S 7(1) of CBA (A.D) Act 1957 or approval of the Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report for acquisition of land for the Project/ Mine in case of outright purchase of land, a census would be undertaken for identification of displaced families and for preparing their socio-economic profile and list of eligible persons for the purpose of receiving Rehabilitation & Resetttlement Benefits.

2. A photo identity card to each Entitled Project Affected Person shall be issued under the signature of the Collector / project authority concerned indicating the following particulars:

(a) Name of the village/GP/PS:

(b) Name, Father's name and address of the head of the family;

(c) Category of entitlement

(d) Whether S.C./S.T./O.B.C./General:

(e) Age, Sex, educational qualification of the members of the family

3. ENTITLEMENT (AS PER R&R POLICY OF CIL, 2012):

Only a 'Displaced' family / person shall be eligible for resettlement benefits.

A family/person shall be termed 'displaced' and hence eligible for resettlement benefits if such family/person has been a permanent resident and ordinarily residing in the project area on the date of publication of notification U/S 9 of CBA(A&D) 1957 / U/S 11 of LA Act, 1894/ Or both/ on the date of the land vested with the State/ Central government as the case may be.

and

on account of acquisition of his/her homestead land / structure is displaced from such areas.

Or

He/she is a homestead-less or landless family/person who has been/is required to be displaced.

A proposal is to be initiated by the unit Authority stating purpose and details of the proposed rehabilitation program commensurate with the provision and timeline envisaged in the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report.

The proposal should contain following information and documents:





| i) Names and locations of villages to be shifted; |
| --- |
| ii) How and when the census of PAFs was carried out, Date of report of the census; |
| iii) Number of Project Affected Families (PAFs) to be rehabilitated as per census report; |
| iv) Whether PAFs are to be resettled at any rehabilitation site or are to be shifted with payment of one-time lumpsum monetary compensation as per R&R Policy of CIL, |
| v) If PAFs are to be resettled at a rehabilitation site then, location, total land area required for the said rehabilitation site and plot area to be allotted to each PAF, |
| vi) Whether land required for the rehabilitation site has been approved and status of acquisition and possession of the same |
| v) Number of Houses, other Structures and assets in the said villages with detailed specification of the said assets and valuation thereof, |
| vi) Whether valuation of assets has been done as per the standard valuation method prescribed in the existing R&R Policy of Coal India Limited? if not then why? (Attach document in support of deviation, if any) |
| vii) Document that furnishes authentic/ approved SOR/ Rate charts etc. which have been considered for valuation of assets; |
| viii) Estimated amount to be paid on account of one-time lumpsum monetary compensation, if any ix) Total capital involvement for R&R of the PAFs along with calculation details, |
| x) Copies of relevant pages of the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual Report along with the order of approval by competent authority |
| xi) Details of deviation with respect to provision of prevalent R&R policy of CIL and relaxation sought, if any |
| xii) Whether full or part of the land is included in 'Raniganj Master Plan' or any such prevalent plan approved by the Central/ State Government for which concerned title holder is to be rehabilitated under the specific terms and conditions as approved by the Central/ State Government; |
| xiii) A revenue plan showing existing villages to be shifted and proposed rehabilitation site for the PAFs indicating land owned by ECL, Government Land, Patta land and land proposed to be acquired washed in different colors. |
| xiv) Checklist CL3 and any other additional document as may be required according to the said Checklist. |
| The proposal is to be examined and recommended by the 'Area Land Cell Committee' and by the Area General Manager. Then the same is to be forwarded to General Manager (LRE) for further processing. |
| General Manager (LRE) will forward the proposal to General Manager (Project & Planning) and General Manager (Finance) for examination and recommendation |
| After that, General Manager (LRE) will forward the proposal with his recommendation to the Functional Directors for their consent |
| Finally, General Manager (LRE) will produce the proposal to CMD, ECL for his approval. If any of the technical or financial parameter of the proposal crosses the limit provided in the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report or any relaxation of provisions of prevalent R&R policy of CIL is required then, the said approval is to be obtained from the Board of Directors of ECL. |
| After obtaining above approval, Area Authority may proceed for payment of value of assets and other R&R benefits strictly in terms of existing provisions of law and other terms and conditions mentioned in the existing R&R Policy and the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report and the specific approval obtained for the purpose. R&R benefits against the homesteads and/ or other assets will be provided to the owners only after thorough verification of ownership records and ensuring that the existing structure has been taken into possession for use of the company / demolished. The operation of taking possession/ demolition of the structure is to be photographed/ video-graphed and should be preserved for future reference. A copy of the said photograph/ relevant still frame of the video is to be attached submitted to Area Finance Manager for certifying payment of asset value and/ or other R&R benefits to the owner of the asset. |
