PROCESS FLOW FOR ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER RIGHT TO FAIR COMPENSATION AND TRANSPARENCY IN LAND ACQUISITION, REHABILITATION AND RESETTEMENT ACT, 2013

(This process flow is according to existing statutory provision, guidelines of MOC as on the date of approval of the SOP etc. Process may change to comply with any modification/ change in statute or guideline that may prevail on the date of actual submission of application for acquisition of land under the aforementioned Act)

1. Acquisition of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 will be done by Gazette Notifications according to the statutory provisions and procedures as laid down in the Act and quantum of land to be acquired should be limited to the provision of the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report of the Mine/ Project.

2. If particulars of the land to be acquired under RFCTLARR Act, 2013 is not clearly mentioned in the approved Project Report/ Scheme/ Conceptual report then, a specific approval of CMD ECL is to be obtained by initiating a proposal.

3. Schedule of the land and graphical representation of the same on Revenue/ Mouza map are to be prepared for acquisition under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

4. Above schedule is to be verified with computerized and physical records of the concerned collieries to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier.

5. Above schedule is to be reconciled with land records of neighboring mines/ Areas in 'FORM-VII' to ensure that any of the plot area has not been acquired by ECL earlier.

6. If rehabilitation of Project Affected Families is proposed then all the information and documents as mentioned in CHECK LIST NO. 3 as mentioned are to be furnished and requisite documents to substantiate the information are to be attached.

7. Proposal is to be initiated by the mine management (signed by Surveyor, land dealing official, safety officer, Project officer/ Agent) and will be forwarded to the General Manager of the concerned Area attaching documents as per CHECK LIST NO. 1.3 for obtaining competent approval with respect to –

a. Acquisition of scheduled land under the said Act.

b. Processing and submitting application to the District land Acquisition Authority for acquisition of the land.

c. Approximate requirement of fund that will be needed to meet 'Cost of Acquisition' which will be demanded by the Land acquisition Authority afterwards and any other cost associated to it.

d. Capturing satellite image of the total acquired land including the settlements/villages which lie within the proposed area of acquisition immediately after Notification with respect to declaration of acquisition by the State Government u/s 19.

8. The land acquisition proposal along with the rehabilitation programme is to be examined and recommended by 'Area Land Cell' (in FORM- XXII) and by the Area General Manager. Then it should be forwarded to General Manager (LRE), Headquarter.

9. General Manager (LRE) will get the proposal examined and vetted by the General Manager (Planning), General Manager (Safety) and General Manager (Finance) In-charge. Then the proposal is to be forwarded for consent of Director (Technical)



Project & Planning, Director (Technical) Operation, Director (Finance) and approval of CMD, ECL

10. After obtaining approval of competent authority, Area General Manager will submit formal application along with required plans and documents to District Land Acquisition Authority for acquisition of specified quantum of land under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

11. Area General Manager will authorize suitable executive (s) who will remain actively involved in the land acquisition activities, monitor progress of the same and intimate it to Area General Manager, keep liaison with the Land acquisition and State/ National monitoring authorities, attend the related meetings, participate in the proceedings held before the Collector/Court for determining the amount of compensation and to protect the interests of the Company by adducing evidence and put across views/ objections of ECL wherever necessary in those meetings. He will communicate Names, designations, places of posting and contact details of those executives to the Collector along with the application.

12. Procedure for acquisition of land, rehabilitation and resettlement of PAFs etc. will be followed as laid down in the Act. However, Area General Manager will take prompt action to deposit the amount demanded by Land acquisition Authority on account of ‘Cost of Acquisition’ within stipulated time.

13. After publication of Notification under section 19 Area General Manager will send written request to General Manager (P&P) and General Manager (Systems) attaching required plan and other information like latitude/ longitude of the acquired land including the settlements/ villages which lie within the area of acquisition for capturing Satellite image of the same area.

14. Executives/ team, authorized to monitor the acquisition process, will examine and ensure that resettlement benefit is limited to summary of R&R published u/s 19 as well as the satellite image captured immediately after it.

15. If employment is generated and the same is admissible to any 'affected family' then employment proposal may be processed after completion of the total acquisition process and possession of land is handed over by the State Government Authority to ECL.

16. Landowner/ 'Affected Family' will submit application in Form-I claiming employment along with all the documents mentioned in Activity Step 2. Processing of employment proposal will be as per activity steps 4,5,6 and 7.



#  IMPORTANT STEPS IN ACQUISITION OF LAND UNDER CBA (A&D) ACT, 1957

Proposal is moved by the General Manager of the concerned Area for acquisition of land under the said Act attaching Statements and certificates in Form 1A, 1B, 1C and 1D regarding availability of coal, verification of land details as per State Government records, that no mineral lease has been obtained earlier on the said area, Draft Notification etc. After obtaining approval of the proposal by competent authority online Application is uploaded in the specified Central Government Portal for publication of Notification in 'Gazette of India' u/s 4 of the Act

Notification u/s 4(1): Publication of preliminary notification for prospecting purpose

Notification u/s 7(1): Notice showing Intention of the Central Government to acquire part or whole area of the land notified u/s 4 for coal mining. Plot-wise details are provided in the notification. Maximum time allowed between notification u/s 4 and 7 is Two Years. Central Government may extend this period up to further one year.

Notification u/s 8(1): Any 'person interested' may lodge a complaint over any portion of land notified u/s 7(1) within thirty days of the said Notification. Notified 'Competent Authority' (Coal Controller at present). Will dispose off the complaints after hearing and his specific recommendation for decision of the Central Government

Notification u/s 9(1): Declaration by the Central Government regarding acquisition of the notified land area

Section 10: By virtue of this section the land notified u/s 9(1) is vested absolutely in the Central Government encumbrance free

Notification u/s 11 (1): Notice for vesting the land, which was vested in the Central Government by virtue of Section 10, is vested in the Government Company.

Notice u/s 12: Power of the competent authority (notified u/s 3) to take possession of the land. He will give notice to the landowner/ ‘person interested’ to hand over possession of the land within such period as he deem fit. In case of any hindrance law and order machinery may be used.



Section 13(5): compensation to be paid to the 'person interested' based on the market value and other damages in the law.

As per Guideline of Central Government: Compensation amount u/s 13 should consist of (a) Market value of land as on the date of notification u/s 4, (b) Solatium @ 30% on the market value of land (c) Interest amount @ 12% for the period between notification u/s 4 and 9 (Max. three years) + @ 9% for one year after notification u/s 9 and @ 15% thenafter till payment is made

Section 14(1): Payment of compensation may be made as per agreed rate. If no agreement could be reached, the matter may be resolved by award given by a Tribunal specially formed for the purpose (For ECL it is at Godda Jharkhand)

Section 17: Central Government has to pay compensation to the ‘person interested’. By Gazette Notification, S.O. 2378 dated $13^{th}$ October 2017 Central Government has delegated this power to CIL and its subsidiaries

Gazette Notification 2368(E) Dated 28th August 2015 [RFCTLARR (Removal of Difficulty Order)]:Central Government has Notified that Compensation and R&R benefits for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 has to be provided as per First, Second and Third Schedule of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.This is effected from 1st September 2015.

As per clarification provided by MoC:

1. Collector is the competent Authority for determination of compensation u/s 26 of RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

2. If determination of compensation has been done before the cut-off date (1-9-15) then the same cannot be reopened.

3. Fresh agreement with ‘affected family’ u/s 14(1) may be executed if it is more beneficial to him.
