WHEREAS:-

A. The First Party is a Government Company primarily engaged in production of coal

B. In order to enhance its coal production, the First Party intends to procure $\_$ Acres of land in $\_$ Mouza from the Government of West Bengal (hereinafter referred to as the "State Government") for expansion of its existing projects/ mines and/ or for developing new projects thereon.

C. The Second Party is in possession of land measuring ______ hectares in Mouza ______, JL No. ______ P.S. District ______ Burdwan, (hereinafter referred to as "the Said Land") which has been granted to the Second Party by the State Government in the nature of 'Patta'.

D. The Second Party is willing to relinquish his right title and interest to the said Land in favour of the State Government and on such relinquishment the First Party is desirous of applying to the State Government for transfer of the Said Land in its name to enable the First Party to expand its business and/ or set up new projects thereon.

E. The parties have agreed to sign and execute this Agreement for recording the terms and conditions agreed upon between the parties.

Now it is hereby agreed and declared between the Parties and this Agreement

witnesseth as follows:

1. Subject to and in accordance with the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement, the Second Party agrees that it shall relinquish and surrender to the State Government, his entire right, title and interest to the Said Land being Patta Land measuring ______ Acres in Plot No. ______, Mouza ______ more fully described in the Schedule hereto and thereon bordered "RED" and for such purpose, shall within ______ days from the date of this Agreement, submit an application to the State Government in accordance with the terms and conditions stipulated by the State Government for cancellation of the 'Patta' granted by the State Government in his favour and also relinquishing and surrendering his right, title and interest to the Said Land in favour of the State Government.

2. On acceptance of the application for surrender, relinquishment and cancellation of 'Patta' submitted by the Second Party to the State Government in terms of Clause 1 hereof, the Second Party shall hand over peaceful, vacant and encumbrance-free possession of the Said Land to the State Government after following the rules, regulations and procedure prescribed by the State Government for such purpose.

3. The Second Party further agrees and undertakes to sign and execute all necessary documents for relinquishment and surrender of his right, title and interest to the Said Land and undertakes to cooperate with the relevant Department/ Ministry of the State Government for the purpose of effecting such relinquishment and all costs, charges and expenses for such relinquishment shall be paid and borne by the Second Party.


4. On cancellation of the 'Patta' granted by the State Government pursuant to relinquishment and surrender of the right, title and interest to the Said Land by the Second Party, the First Party shall be entitled to apply to the State Government for transfer of the Said land in its name in accordance with the provisions contained in the Memo No. 67/S (reg ECL)/17 dated 11/01/2017 published by the Government of West Bengal, Office of the District Land and Land Reforms Officer, Burdwan for the purpose of expansion of its business and/ or for setting up new projects thereon and the Second Party shall not raise any objection to such application being made by the First Party to the State Government.

5. If the Patta land of the Second Party is less than 2 acres, the Second Party shall be entitled to club the area of the Said land being relinquished by him in terms of this Agreement with land being relinquished by other Patta holders in the said area and also with freehold land transferred to the First Party by land owners in the said area so that the total area of land being relinquished by the Patta holders to the State Government clubbed together with land transferred by the said land owners and / or other Patta holders to the First Party is at least equal to minimum area of land required for one employment as per prevalent R&R Policy and Norms of the First Party and the Second Party together with such Patta holders / land owners shall be entitled to jointly nominate one person for employment in the company of the First Party in terms of Clause 6 hereof.

6. For every minimum area of land required for one employment as per prevalent R&R Policy and Norms of the First Party consisting of Patta land being relinquished and surrendered to the State Government and/or freehold land transferred to the First Party respectively as the case may be , the Second Party shall be entitled, either alone or together with the other patta holders and/ or other and owners in the said area, to request for employment for one person nominated by them in the company of the First Party by submitting the application form prescribed by the First Party duly filled in, together with the Patta Certificate or title deeds as the case may be and other documents evidencing their right, title and interest to the patta land / freehold land and also the application submitted by the Patta holders to the State Government for relinquishment of right, title and interest to the Patta land. The First Party shall give employment to the person nominated by the Second Party together with the other patta holders and land owners only on transfer and handing over possession of the minimum area of land required for one employment by the State Government and the said land owners in its favour in accordance with the Rehabilitation and Resettlement Policy of Coal India Limited (R&R Policy), the Service Rules and all Employment Norms and Guidelines of the First Party.

7. Within __ days from the date of execution of this Agreement, the Second Party shall submit to the First Party, an Affidavit affirmed before the First Class Magistrate, inter-alia stating therein the name of the person nominated for employment in the company of the First Party, the total area of land being relinquished and affirming the genuiness of all copies of documents submitted by the Second Party for employment of the person nominated by him in the company of the First Party and also agreeing to be bound by the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement and the Rehabilitation and Resettlement Policy of Coal India Limited (R&R Policy) and also the Service Rules and all Employment Norms and Guidelines of the First Party.

8. This Agreement shall not be terminated by either Party to this Agreement for any reason whatsoever.
