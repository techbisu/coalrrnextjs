#  Note for the 330th Meeting of the Board of Directors of ECL

Policy decision regarding providing employment to the recorded 'Sebaits' of private Debottar Land, which has either been acquired under any land acquisition law or purchased through registration of sale deed relaxing the provision of the norm that restricts providing employment without mutation of land.

Kunustoria Area initiated a proposal seeking approval for providing relaxation in self-imposed norm of ECL for providing employment to the recorded Sebaits of 13.71 Acres of already purchased and used private Debottar land for Egara OC Patch.

Scheme for Egara OC Patch was approved by ECL Board in its $296^{th}$ meeting held on $18^{th}$ March, 2017. In the said scheme total requirement of land was projected as 67.09 acres. However, the same was reassessed later as 66.96 acres and schedule of the land was approved by the competent authority vide Note Sheet Ref. No. ECL/CMD/LRE/Kunustoria Area/805 dated $4^{th}$ August, 2017. Kunstoria Area has mentioned in the proposal that, they have already purchased 31.86 acres of tenancy land according to said schedule, which includes 13.71 acres of ‘Debottar’ land (Land recorded in the names of ‘Deity’).

Revenue Officer, office of BL&LRO, Raniganj conducted detailed enquiry regarding the said land and it has been mentioned in the 'Order Sheet' that (Copy enclosed as Annexure-A):

a) The Debottar land is private in nature;

b) The property is set apart for worship of family deity in which public has no role;

c) The beneficial interest is vested upon ‘Sebait’ only.

A legal opinion was also obtained by the Area Authority from Advocate Pijush Kanti Das for purchase of the scheduled Debottar land. It has been mentioned in the said opinion that,

"The Sebait of the deity have got every transferable right of Debottar land as an individual 'Rayat' for the benefit of the deity and such condition is required to be recited in the sale deed." (Copy enclosed as Annexure-B).

Accordingly, Project management has purchased 13.71 acres of ‘Debottar’ land from concerned Sebaits. But mutation of the same in favour of ECL is pending at the office of BL&LRO, Raniganj.

However, the matter of purchase and mutation of Debottar land is subjected to various legal complications as on date. Instant proposal cannot be dealt as an isolated case only because it addresses a policy decision regarding continuation of providing employment to 'Sebait' of a private Debottar property recognizing him as a 'Project Affected People' under R&R Policy of CIL for not only the land purchased through registration of sale deed but also for possession of land under involuntary acquisition through other Acts like CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, Land Acquisition Acts etc. Details with respect to meaning, types, legal ownership status of Debottar Property and salient points of the legal opinions received in this regard have been described in (Copy enclosed as Annexure-C).

However, gist of the same including recent actions taken by ECL and developments in this regard is given below:

a) Private Debottar lands are owned by a deity of particular person or family. There is no public involvement with the said temple/ deity. Generally, a private sebait is appointed for management of the same.

b) Joint Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal has communicated a 'Record of Discussion' with respect to the meeting held in this regard with Land Reform Commissioner on 17th August, 2018. It is mentioned in the said communication that, "In respect


of Debottar Property the existing provisions of law and orders of Hon'ble High Court shall have to be followed." In the attached Order of Hon'ble Judge Mr. Jyotirmoy Bhattacharya, dated 22nd August, 2016 with respect an appeal suit [S.A.T. 195 of 2016 and CAN 6710 of 2016] it has been mentioned, "The suit property was dedicated to the deity. Deity is the Perpetual minor. Sale of any Debottar property without permission of the District Judge concerned is void" (Copy enclosed as Annexure-D).

c) It is worth mentioning here that, before this communication ECL used to purchase Debottar property from the recorded 'Sebaits' and many of the purchased property had been recorded in favour of ECL by the State revenue department also. But State revenue department has changed its stand now. Online mutation module warrants a reference of court case order for completing the process. Director of Land Records & Survey & Joint Land Reforms Commissioner, West Bengal has stated in his letter to the Principal Secretary and LRC, West Bengal, Memo. Ref. No. 9/640/C/18 dated 28th March, 2018, and has recommended withdrawal of previous circular wherein it was opined that 'Transfer of Debottar land by sebaits cannot in all cases be prima facie termed as illegal" (Copy enclosed as Annexure-E).

d) Legal opinions of Justice Ashim Kumar Banerjee, retired Chief Justice (Acting) of Calcutta High Court and Mr. Shiv Shankar Banerjee, an empanelled advocate at Calcutta High Court and Supreme Court have also been obtained this regard. From the said legal opinions it is revealed that, providing employment against purchase/acquisition of Debottar land is not supported by provision of law, although, till now, private Debottar land used to be purchased from recorded 'Sebaits' providing him land value and employment benefit as per prevalent R&R Policy because landowner is non-living entity and 'Sebait' is recognised as a 'PAP' for the acquired purchased property (Copy enclosed as Annexure-F).

e) Few recorded Sebaits have filed petitions in the court of District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman seeking permission for alienation of the concerned Debottar Lands. Recently, one Order has been passed (Order No. 04 dated $6^{th}$ February, 2020) by Hon'ble District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman, Sri Kausik Bhattacharyya, in the Misc. Case No. 71 of 2019 of Swapan Kumar Mondal & 16 Ors. Vs A.D.M. & D.L. & L.R.O. & 01 Anr. (Copy enclosed as Annexure-G). In the said Order various provisions of law regarding transfer of Debottar land have been critically analysed and it has been finally concluded that,

"Borrowing of wisdom from the above decision of the Hon'ble Court, I do conclude that this court has no scope to grant permission in the matter of alienation of private Debottar property by the Sebaits. This Misc. Case is, therefore, not maintainable and it shall be dismissed".

Similar Orders (Order No. 04 dated $6^{th}$ February, 2020 and Order No. 05 dated $7^{th}$ February, 2020) have been passed by Hon'ble District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman in Misc. Case No 70 of 2019 and 72 of 2019 also.

The Orders are general in nature and has been passed by the authority, who is the custodian of minors (Deity being a juristic person and a perpetual minor as per the above interpretation of the State Authority).

f) In view of the above orders, General Manager (LRE) has submitted application to the Additional District Magistrate and District Land and Land Reforms Officer, Paschim Bardhaman, letter Ref. No. ECL/CMD/LRE/1927 dated $12^{th}$ February, 2020, (Copy enclosed as Annexure-H) requesting him to mutate private Debottar lands, which have been purchased or will be purchased by ECL under his Jurisdiction and stating that,

"As no permission can be granted by the District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman with respect to transfer of Debottar Land, ECL will be constrained to purchase land directly from the recorded Sebaits and will take possession of Debottar Land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957 providing him Compensation,


employment and R&R benefits to the concerned Sebaits cognizing him the 'person interested'".

g) The matter was discussed in the meeting with Principal Secretary and LRC, West Bengal on 26th February, 2020 and with other Authorities at District level. However, it is anticipated that the procedural formalities of the State Government for resolving the issue may take substantial time; because they will possibly obtain opinions of legal authorities including Advocate General, West Bengal before arriving at any decision regarding mutation of Debottar land.

h) In case of land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957,

i) The land is vested free from all encumbrances in the Central government on the date of publication of Notice under section 9 of the said. Said land is transferred to the Government Company (in this case, ECL) free from all encumbrances by Notification under section 11 of the said Act and ECL becomes title holder of the land from the date of publication of said Notice.

ii) As per existing practice, ECL takes possession of Debottar land, which has been declared as private by the State Revenue Department, by paying land compensation and providing Rehabilitation and Resettlement benefits including employment as per provisions of R&R policy of CIL to the recorded 'sebait' of the said land.

It is revealed from the present situation that continuation of providing employment, as per prevalent R&R Policy of CIL, to ‘Sebait’ of a private Debottar property for obtaining possession of the same may become necessary due to following reasons:

1. Proposed employments are within the limit approved by Competent Authority/ECL Board in corresponding Project Report or scheme etc. of particular mine/project.

2. It is a present practice and Sebaits will not be interested to handover possession of the property to ECL if he is deprived of the employment benefit. Projects are likely to be severely affected if Debottar properties could not be possessed in time.

3. In some cases, Project Management has even used the concerned Debottar land. ‘Sebaits’ of Debottar properties are already showing their resentments and agitations in different projects like Egara OCP, Jhanjra U/G Project and Sonepur Bazari OCP for not providing employment benefits against acquisition of associated properties.

4. Employment benefit is provided to land-loser under provisions of R&R Policy of CIL. There is no bar in the said policy to extend the benefit to 'Sebaits'. In the eligibility criteria for 'Economic Rehabilitation Benefits' in clause 6 it has been mentioned that "This benefit shall accrue only to Entitled Project Affected Person (PAP)". Entitled Project Affected Person shall be one from the following categories:

i. Persons from whom land is acquired including tribal cultivating land under traditional rights.

ii. Persons whose homestead is acquired.

iii. Sharecroppers, land losers, tenants & day labourers.

iv. Tribal dependent on forest produce as certified by the District Forest Officer/Revenue Authorities."

So, the R&R Policy of CIL recommends providing R&R benefits even to Sharecroppers, land-less people, tenants & day labourers also. ‘Sebait’, who is the only human being, who is entitled to enjoy the rights to use a piece of Debottar land keeping the same in his possession through generations, owes to be compensated properly for loss of livelihood caused by alienation of associated land, may also be treated as ‘Project Affected People’ under the same principle.


5. State Government has directed to abide by the legal provision i.e. to obtain permission of District Judge, before transfer of Debottar land. But, Hon'ble District Judge, Paschim Bardhaman, has disposed off the petitions of Sebaits of private Debottar land stating that the same is not maintainable and decisions making procedure for resolving the issue by the State Government may take substantial time that will eventually adversely affect operations of various mining projects of ECL.

6. In case of land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, title of the land is already with ECL. But the possession is with the concerned Sebait. Obviously, he will not handover possession of the land to ECL if the company does not compensate his loss of livelihood. Spirit of the above Orders of the District Judge appears to be in coherence with identification of 'Sebait' as a 'person interested' for a private Debottar land acquired under the said Act and receiving R&R benefits from ECL.

7. In the 'System Improvement Norm' approved in the $310^{th}$ meeting of ECL Board held on $10^{th}$ April, 2018. In the said norm it has been mentioned as follows (Copy enclosed as Annexure-I):

a) Ownership Certificate is mandatory for purchase of land. Land can be purchased through sale deed by ECL from a landowner only if his name is recorded as owner against the concerned plot area of land in the records of State Revenue Department.

b) Mutation of land in favour of ECL must be done before providing employment / compensation except in cases where land transfer is governed/restricted by SPT/CNT Act or where conditional employments have been approved by the Competent Authority against land which are disputed/sub-judice and mutation of land in favour of the company is not possible before providing employment due to statutory/legal obligations, or the land has already been acquired through CBA(A&D) Act, 1957 and the mutation hasn't been done in view of clarification of Ministry of Coal, Government of India through letter Ref. No. 43024/6/99-PRIW dated 2nd November, 2007.

8. So, the above norm, as such, does not restrict providing employment to land loser for land acquired under CBA (A&D) Act, 1957. Consent of ECL Board may be required only with respect to continuation of practice of providing employments to sebaits of private Debottar land (as ‘Person interested’ of the said land) in the present environment and stand of the State Government.

9. Although current judgement of the Court does not bar payment of land compensation to the ‘Sebait’ but in view of the decisions pending by the State Government regarding mutation, payment of the same, for those cases where it has not been paid till now, may be withheld at this moment and the same may be regularized after obtaining affirmative decision of the State Government.

10.The proposal has been examined and vetted by General Manager (Project & Planning) and General Manager (Finance) I/C. General Manager (Legal) has stated in his comment that,

"This is purely administrative decision and to be dealt administratively and for the greater interest of the company."

11. Subsidiary Board has been empowered by CIL to take policy decision on compensation and R&R issues based on unique conditions and ground realities for long term interest of the company vide letter Ref. No. CIL/DT/R&R/1437 dated $13^{th}$ December, 2019 and CIL/PMD/LR/392 dated $8^{th}$ August, 2018 (Copy enclosed as Annexure-J).

The proposal was agreed and recommended by Director (Technical) Project & Planning and Director (Finance) and approved by Chairman-cum-Managing Director for placing before the Empowered Committee of Functional Directors. The proposal was placed as Item No. 05 in the meeting of the Empowered Committee of Functional Directors. ($5^{th}$ of


2020) held on $11^{th},$ $14^{th}$ and $16^{th}$ May, 2020 and Functional Directors have recommended for placing the proposal before ECL Board for approval (Copy enclosed as Annexure-K).

In view of the above, the proposal of policy decision regarding providing employment to the recorded 'Sebaits' of private Debottar Land, which has either been acquired under any land acquisition law or purchased through registration of sale deed relaxing the provision of the norm that restricts providing employment without mutation of land, is placed before the Board for consideration and accord of approval.

As per recommendation of Chief Vigilance Officer, Eastern Coalfields Limited, Competent authority has been pleased to approve following procedure for physical verification of relationship of landowner with the nominee regarding his claim of employment against land:

1. General Manger of the Area should request In-charge of local Police Station, attaching related documents submitted by Landowner/ nominee with respect to their relationship, to physically verify the same and certify whether the relationship claimed in the documents are genuine.

The team will visit the residential place of the landowner and physically judge relationship between the landowner and nominee. The team will take a written and duly signed statement of the landowner in this regard on the spot and the same should be countersigned by two neighboring villagers as witnesses and the team members mentioning full address of the place and date.

Above procedure should be followed strictly before initiation of any employment proposal for approval with immediate effect and copy of the certificate regarding physical verification of the relationship must be attached to the concerned employment proposal. This measure is to be carried out in addition to the existing procedure of document based relationship verification procedure.

The following provisions in respect of system improvement regarding Resettlement and Rehabilitation Policy of ECL, as suggested by the Vigilance department and duly approved by the Competent Authority, shall be adhered to and followed for carrying out resettlement and rehabilitation related activities in ECL.

##  Norms w.r.t System Improvement

In order to prevent the re-occurrence of the irregularities, as has been observed during an enquiry conducted by the Vigilance Department, ECL on the system and procedure as was followed for finalization of R&R benefits in respect of Bhadotola Village of Rajmahal Area, a system improvement regarding implementation of the existing provisions of Resettlement & Rehabilitation Policy 2012 of CIL has been suggested as under:

A. The provisions of Resettlement & Rehabilitation Policy 2012 of CIL with respect to rehabilitation of project affected people (PAPs) should be followed in all projects of Eastern Coalfields Limited and formation of committee for census and settlement of compensation should be done as per provisions of clause-9 of Resettlement & Rehabilitation Policy 2012 of CIL which speaks as below:

Clause-9 Resettlement & Rehabilitation Committee-A committee will be constituted at project level under the chairmanship of the collector to be called the Rehabilitation and Resettlement Committee with the following objectives to monitor and review the progress of implementation of the Rehabilitation and Resettlement Scheme and to carry out post implementation social audits in consultation with the village panchayat in rural areas and municipality in urban areas in the manner will be detailed by the concerned State Govt.:

I. To approve the list of land losers and other PAPs;

II. To approve the list of persons eligible to be offered employment as per R&R Policy;

III. To approve the detailed Rehabilitation Plan for the project in consultation with the displaced persons and Gram Sabhas;

IV. To expedite issue of domicile certificate and other necessary documentation required for State Authorities;

V. To monitor and review the progress of the Rehabilitation Scheme, grant of benefits and handing over of possession of land in a smooth manner;

VI. To facilitate the land acquisition process in any other manner as may be required including resolution of disputes;

VII. To carry out post implementation social audit in consultation with the authorities.

Sub.: Minutes of the 296th Meeting of Board of Directors of ECL, held on 18th March, 2017.

Reproduced below is the relevant extracts from the Minutes of the 296th Meeting of the Board of Directors of Eastern Coalfields Limited, held on 18th March, 2017 with regard to the following:

"296.03(A) Abolition of three self-imposed norms of ECL to facilitate smooth acquisition/taking physical possession of land for different projects/existing mines &

296.03(X) Relaxation of the two self-imposed norms of ECL for more than two nominations by a landowner and more than two employments for a family against loss of their land for Jhanjra Project, ECL.

(i) Director (Personnel) apprised the Board that in the past five years, Govt. of West Bengal ceased to involve itself in acquisition of land. So, acquisition of land through LA Act, 1894 (or, RFCTLARR Act, 2013 as on date) has virtually been stopped in West Bengal. Owing to abnormal delay in the acquisition process, ECL requested the State Govt. to close pending cases of acquisition through LA Act and the State Authority closed most of the pending LA cases and in many cases deposited money was also refunded after deduction of 10% administrative charge. In the above scenario of land acquisition, wherein lion's share has been credited to outright purchase, ECL is now facing the following situation:

(1) Landowner has now choice of not selling his land to ECL;

(2) ECL has to purchase land only from last recorded landowner irrespective of the person's period of ownership over the land and without consideration of gender of the person;

(3) ECL has to purchase land for running projects/mines even from the landowners who have already nominated two nominees in the past against loss of land. But, now they are reluctant to sell their land as they know that they are not eligible for employment.

(4) ECL generally pays land value to the tune of ₹ 2-3 Lakh/Acre with employment to the landowner at present. This land value is less than the present market value of land. Again, as per existing norm, one family/landowner can get only two employments against loss of land. This has created dissatisfaction among landowners, who feel that they are being underpaid especially against the land for which they are not entitled to get employment. So, they try to sell their land to others at market price/at higher value. But, there also his eligibility of getting employment is restricted by the norm of 'minimum three year's ownership.'

(5) Area Authorities are facing tremendous problems in acquiring total land required for the entire project area as some of the land owners are creating substantial resistance and in some cases, land owners are getting united for not selling their land in protest of non-availability of employment opportunity due to existence of some self-imposed norms of ECL.

In case of land already acquired through CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, ECL has to take physical possession of the land from the landowner, who has legal right, title and ownership as on the date of notification u/s 9 of the said Act by paying him monetary compensation as per


statutory provision and other R&R benefits including employment as per provisions of R&R Policy of CIL. Being involuntary acquisition, the concerned landowner has no power to refuse the transfer of the title but he has every right to bargain on the compensation package for handing over physical possession to ECL under sec. 14(1) of the said Act. Decision of not providing employment on the basis of self-imposed norms of ECL are creating resentment amongst landowners and projects/mines authorities are facing problem in taking physical possession of land for running their projects/mines. The major three norms which are creating bottlenecks in land acquisition and have been proposed for abolition are:

(1) Requirement of minimum three years' period of ownership of the land owner over a plot of land.

(3) Employment of Females against loss of land.

Three major self-imposed norms, which are creating bottlenecks in purchase of land or taking physical possession of land that has already been acquired through CBA (A&D) Act along with their source and negative impacts on acquisition of land are as follows:

(A) Requirement of minimum three years' period of ownership of the land owner over a land: This norm was included in the draft guideline for providing employment under land loser scheme that was approved by the Functional Directors in their meeting held on $31^{st}$ July, 2000. As per the said guideline, this norm was incorporated to avoid transaction of land with a motive of getting job in ECL. Later in the $176^{th}$ meeting of ECL Board, held on $30^{th}$ March, 2003, the matter was again discussed. In the revised guideline approved in the said meeting of ECL Board, it is mentioned as follows:

After protracted deliberation, Board authorized Chairman to deal the matter."

Impact on Land acquisition:

Projects/mines authorities are constrained to purchase the land wherein landowners having period of ownership less than 3 years prior to the acquisition/ use of the land by ECL for operation of their projects/mines and they are also constrained to forward the employment proposals involving above type of land with recommendation to relax the aforesaid norm.

2) Non-approval of the employment proposal on the ground of aforesaid norm communicates negative signal amongst the landowners, who are prompted not to sell further land to ECL and projects/mines authorities are facing problem in further purchase of land or taking physical possession of land acquired through CBA (A&D) Act, 1957.

As per the available statistics, approximately 37.24% of the total employment approved in last three years involved land having less than three years' ownership of landowners over some portions of land offered for employment. So, it has become almost a routine job of CMD, ECL to approve such employment proposals.

and, ECL has to deal with huge numbers of employment proposals for according his approval with respect to relaxation of aforesaid norm that consumes substantial valuable time of the competent authority for disposing off this type of repetitive job.

Previous instances of relaxation approved: For Sonepur Bazari Project, relaxation of the same norm was already approved by ECL Board, in its $246^{th}$ Meeting, held on $15^{th}$ July, 2011 as well as previous $170^{th}$ and $171^{st}$ meetings, wherein providing employment was approved to and transferees, who purchased their land even after vesting of the land to Central Govt. by virtue of notification u/s 9(i) of CBA (A&D) Act, 1957.

Provision of R&R Policy: There is no provision in the R&R Policy of CIL 2012, wherein other acquisition of land or providing employment against acquired land has been restricted in the basis of ownership period of the landowner over the land.

3) Ineligibility of a land owner to nominate more than two nominees for employments and non-entitlement of a family to get more than two employments against loss of land: In the norms for employment against land that was approved in the 176th ECL Board meeting, held on 30th March, 2003, it was stated that:



"In all cases including tagging and package deal, nomination for employment to maximum of two persons by same owner should be allowed. This is to be implemented everywhere except at those places in Jharkhand State where CNT/SPT Act is applicable."

#  Impact on Land acquisition:

(1) The landowners, who have already transferred their land to ECL and have nominated more than two nominees for employment in the past without having knowledge about the said norm are now showing agitation before the management and are claiming employments.

(2) Landowners having above problem are not willing to transfer/handover physical possession of their land to ECL.

(3) Due to acute problem being faced in taking physical possession of the land acquired through CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, Jhanjra Project has already moved a separate proposal for relaxing the aforesaid norm. Other Areas are also reporting their problems to LRE Department regularly.

(3) In certain cases, more than four acres of land is recorded in the name of head of the family like father or grandfather and there are more than two adult members in the down hierarchy of the family. But, due to acquisition of land through CBA (A&D) Act, 1957, the land cannot be mutated in the names of present adult members of the family. In this case also, total employment is restricted to two only against the undivided family and other adult members are deprived of the benefit. Now, the family members of these types of undivided families are not willing to handover possession of land to ECL.

#  Previous instances of relaxation approved:

(1) The aforementioned norms of ECL have been relaxed for New Kenda Mega Project of Kenda Area by approval of competent authority vide Ref. No. ECL/CMD/LRE/ 150/Area/123, dated 4th February, 2015.

(2) The same norm was relaxed for Sonepur Bazari Project in the $255^{th}$ ECL Board Meeting, held on $4^{th}$ August, 2012 as well.

Provision of R&R Policy: This is a self-imposed norm of ECL and there is no provision in the R&R Policy of CIL 2012.

(C) Non-Employment of Females against loss of land: In the norms for employment against land that was approved in the $176^{th}$ ECL Board meeting, held on $30^{th}$ March, 2003 it was stated that:

"Employment against land should be provided to males only in view of the limited employment opportunity in mining. In exceptional cases where there is no male nominee, the proposal for female employment should be placed before the Board for its consideration."

##  Impact on Land acquisition:

(1) There are lot of female landowners who, in the present socio-economic structure are capable of doing jobs themselves and are demanding employments for themselves;

(2) Many landowners have no son and only daughters. They want to nominate their daughters for employment to safeguard their future;

(3) In many cases, sons of the landowners are already employed and they want to nominate their female descendant for employment;

a) "land owner" includes any person, who is entitled to be granted Patta rights on the land under any law of the State including assigned lands.

##  NOTE

There are the following kinds of lands in the possession / ownership of ECL :-

(1) Lease : The Leases granted by the State Government since the time of erstwhile owners are lease in respect of the underground minerals only, leases do not extend to surface land. Whenever the user of surface land is required, ECL has to occupy the same against payment of surface rent to the State Government. That mining leaseholds as it is stood transferred to ECL by virtue of the Coal Mines Nationalization Act, 1973.

ECL has no authority over the surface land belonging to State Government; after the work is over, the mining lease will revert back to the State Government. Under the provisions of Nationalization Act, ECL is just a lessee having no permanent right, title and interest in the mining leaseholds.

(2) The Land acquired under LA Act : The surface land is acquired by ECL under the provisions of Land Acquisition Act, 1894 mainly for the purpose of Open Cast Mining and for rehabilitation sites or for depillaring purpose. The acquired land vests in ECL absolutely.

ECL has no authority whatsoever to part with the land free of considera-tion which ECL had to acquire against valuable consideration.

(3) Land acquired under CBA Act : Land acquired under CBA Act vests absolutely in ECL and similarly, like the previous instance ECL has no authority to part with the same in favour of anybody whosoever without valuable consideration. The Memorandum and Article of Association of Eastern Coalfields Limited nowhere authorize ECL to part with the land owned by it free of cost.

(4) Purchased Land: The land purchased by ECL from different vendors again vests in ECL and the same cannot be parted with. ECL has no authority to part with the land, by whatever means acquired once it becomes Company's property.

The aforesaid incapacity of ECL can be cured only by enactment of appropriate Law / promulgation of administrative circulars by the Government of India through Coal India Limited; the holding Company.

Minutes of the $164^{th}$ Meeting $(3^{rd}$ of 2001) of the Board of Directors of Eastern Coalfields Limited held on Tuesday, the 10th July, 2001 at the Regd. Office of the Company at Sanctoria at 10:30 A.M.

#  164.04(I) Norms for providing employment.

Director (Personnel) apprised the Board about the R&R Policy, circulated by Coal India Limited in the month of August 2000. The land losers were not responding to the monetary compensation and insisting for the employment. The company requires land for its mining activities and rehabilitation purposes.

The Board observed that so long as the projects/schemes are of self-financing nature i.e. without creating any extra burden on the existing cash-flow of the company and keeping the recruitment to the barest minimum the company may go ahead towards recruitment of land losers by adopting the following guidelines:

1. One employment against 2 acres of land taken from the landowner. The ownership should be absolute and free from any encumbrance or litigation.

2. Land should be taken in possession of the company before/at the time of offering employment.

3. A maximum of two employment against land under one ownership would be admissible where land holding exceeds four acres.

4. In Package Deal, landowners having less than two acres of land maybe permitted to tag his land with other landowners to make it 2 acres for claiming one employment. In such cases, all the land losers/owners will have to forego the right of monetary compensation.

5. Tagging will only be permissible if the landowner holds land within the same package covering the same area. All such landowners will nominate only one person for offer of employment opportunities.

6. No tagging of land will be allowed for land which has already been acquired and belongs to the company.

7. Age will be below 35 years on the date of offering employment opportunity.

8. CMD may be authorised to clear those cases where minor deviation if any is required on emergency basis in case of offer of employment in the


interest of the organisation and for maintaining the production line. The Board will be apprised subsequently.

9. Such opportunity should be provided to the landowners himself or his son or his grandson. In case the landowner has no son, and only daughter, employment will be provided to the son in law if the daughter authorises her husband for such employment.

10. Employment should be confined to the males only in view of the limited employment opportunities in mining except where there is no dependant male.

The Board further authorised the Chairman-cum-Mg. Director to deal with the cases within the approved norm and keep the Board informed, periodically, about the recruitment.
